# Institution-Building Knowledge Base

*Thin-but-reliable, per [[institution-building]]. Each area: what it is · when it enters (decision-step, see [[decision-map]]) · key decisions · typical cost (where known) · major risks · specialist. **Not encyclopedic** — depth lives in the specialists ([[specialist-map]]). Seeded only from what we already know; the rest accretes from cases + conversations. Tags: [DOC]=documented · [SEED]=from a Nariway case/brief · [TBR]=to be researched on demand.*

| Area | When | Key decisions & notes | Cost | Specialist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Structure / entity** | 4 | 501(c)(3); **private operating foundation vs. public charity** (Chapter 42 rules, self-dealing) [DOC]; who owns the collection vs. the building | — | nonprofit/tax attorney |
| **Tax & estate** | 3–4 | Donation rules; qualified appraisal thresholds; estate implications; **model consequences before any transfer** [DOC] | — | tax counsel, T&E attorney |
| **Collection care** | 5 | Inventory, appraisal, authentication, documentation, accessioning; CMS software | CMS ~$29–135/mo [DOC] | registrar, CMS vendor |
| **Risk & insurance** | 5 | Fine-art, general liability, D&O, property, cyber, workers' comp; **profile changes once the public enters** | — | fine-art insurer/broker |
| **Conservation & storage** | 5 | Environmental/climate control, handling, condition reporting, disaster planning; *inputs to the building, not afterthoughts* | — | conservator, storage provider |
| **Logistics / movement** | 5 | Packing, crating, transport, couriers, customs, temporary storage | — | fine-art logistics (e.g. Crozier) |
| **Building** | 5 | **Adaptive reuse vs. purpose-built**; security, HVAC, lighting, fire suppression, accessibility, loading, visitor flow. *McNay: 75% of a house rebuilt, basement excavated for climate control* [SEED] | feasibility ~0.1–0.5% of capital; concept/master-plan $30–100K+ [DOC] | architect, museum planner |
| **Operations** | 6 | Staffing, hours, ticketing, visitor services, security, maintenance, policies | — | museum operations consultant |
| **Governance** | 4 | Board structure, founder control, succession, conflicts, decision rights. *McNay: self-perpetuating board named pre-death → survived founder* [SEED] (H8) | — | nonprofit attorney |
| **Money / funding** | 3 | Capital budget, **annual operating budget → required endowment**, fundraising, earned income, reserves. *McNay: runs on endowment + contributions, not gate; ~9.3× coverage (upper bound)* [SEED] | — | endowment/fundraising advisor |
| **Programming** | 6 | Permanent vs. rotating; loans; education; publications | — | curator |
| **Public responsibility** | all | Accessibility, community relevance, scholarly access, provenance, repatriation, ethics; **AAM accreditation** (≥2 yrs open, ≥1,000 hrs/yr, ≥1 paid professional, 80% accessioned; 8–16 mo process) [DOC] | — | — |
| **Opening** | 7 | Approvals, vendors, systems, policies, people in place before day one | — | owner's rep (Nariway) |
| **Long-term survival** | 8 | Post-founder governance; failure modes yrs 5/10/25. *di Rosa (distress), Terra (collapse) will seed this* [SEED] | — | — |

*Realistic outcome context [DOC, transfer note]: most inherited collections are retained; only ~6% of collectors plan museum donations; new institutions are the rare tail. So the Suitability/Form/Viability screen (steps 1–3) usually matters more than the building.*
