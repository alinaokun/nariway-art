# Specialist Map (guide)

The independent specialist bench of [[institution-building]], the "who can execute it" asset. Records are the notes in this folder; open `specialists.base` for the sortable, filterable view. The killer field is **cases**, the analogous work a specialist has actually done, so that in time Toi can answer a question like "who has worked on a seasonal exhibition foundation in an adaptive-reuse building" from Nariway's own research. Fed by every case packet's *Advisors used* block and by specialist interviews.

## Record schema (fill over time)
`category · expertise · geography · typical client · cases (analogous work done) · interviewed · vetted · tier`

Presence here is a lead, not an endorsement. Vet before any name reaches a client's table.

## Open, high-priority gaps (no vetted name yet)
Nonprofit and tax attorneys, trusts-and-estates attorneys, registrars, conservators, security consultants, endowment and fundraising advisors, community and audience specialists. These are the categories to fill first, since they gate the early decisions in the [[decision-map]].

## Not the bench
Full-lifecycle incumbents (Gurr Johns, The Fine Art Group, Winston Artory Group) are competitors, tracked elsewhere. Some specialists are also relationships in [[partners]]; overlap is fine.

## How it grows
Every case packet's *Advisors used* block drops names here. Specialist interviews add the rich fields, especially cases worked on. The problem-discovery conversations both test the market and populate the bench, the same contacts serving two purposes (see [[problem-discovery]]).
