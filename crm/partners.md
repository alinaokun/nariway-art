# Partners — referral & connection network

*Third-party partners and connections: **referral sources** (who sends Nariway clients) and **specialists** (who executes the work). Fed by [[nariway-signals]]. Related but distinct from [[specialist-map]] (that's the execution bench for building institutions; this is the broader relationship/referral network — with overlap). **These same people — estate attorneys, family offices, insurers — are the [[problem-discovery]] targets, so growing this register grows the discovery pipeline.** Professional/public information only.*

## Referral channels, by priority (from the founding brief)
*The business is referral-distributed — ~20–30 productive relationships beat hundreds of collector prospects.*

| Channel | Value | Why | Doubles as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trusts & estates attorneys** | Very high | Encounter collection succession at the moment complexity becomes unavoidable | discovery conversation |
| **Multi-family offices** | Very high | Natural users of the hybrid model; one relationship → several families | discovery conversation |
| **Private wealth advisors / private banks (no art team)** | Very high | Own the client, don't want to own every specialist | discovery conversation |
| **CPAs / private-client tax partners** | High | Gifts, estates, basis, charitable events reveal the problem | — |
| **Fine-art insurance brokers** | High | See outdated schedules, poor records, risk issues | discovery conversation |
| **Appraisers** | High | Discover broader planning issues they don't want to handle | specialist bench |
| **Auction-house specialists** | Medium | Valuable at disposition, but transaction-motivated | — |
