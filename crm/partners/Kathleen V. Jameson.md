## Who / why
**Kathleen V. Jameson, Ph.D.** — Director, **Speed Art Museum**, Louisville, KY, appointed September 2026. A museum leader, scholar and arts advocate with more than three decades of experience across museum leadership, curatorial work, fundraising, strategic planning and community engagement, and a scholar of late-19th- and early-20th-century American art (photography and works on paper). She joins the Speed from the James A. Michener Art Museum in Bucks County, PA, where she was executive director from 2018; earlier she spent seven years as CEO of the Mint Museum in Charlotte, NC, and held executive positions at the Museum of Fine Arts, Houston. Her appointment is a homecoming of sorts — she earned her master's in art history at the University of Louisville and served as the Speed's own directorial intern and research assistant in 1995, working under former director Peter Morrin. She steps in as the Speed approaches its centennial in 2027. **Public-source lead — no contact yet.**
