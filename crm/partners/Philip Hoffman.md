## Who / why
**Philip Hoffman** — Founder and CEO, **The Fine Art Group**, one of the world's leading independent art advisory and finance firms per the *Financial Times*. He began his career as finance director at Christie's at 27, becoming the youngest member of its management board at 33, before founding The Fine Art Group, which now advises ultra-high-net-worth collectors, family offices, sovereign wealth structures and major private banks globally and manages roughly 350 family-office art collections. Under his direction the firm has built strategies with family offices, advisors and nonprofits for converting art holdings into compliant charitable structures — donations, transfers and related solutions. He is a frequently quoted public voice on the generational art-wealth-transfer thesis specifically: told Bloomberg, on the roughly $1 trillion in art expected to change hands over the next decade, that "the next gen don't want it," a direct articulation of the exact tension Nariway's thesis is built around. **Public-source lead — no contact yet.**
