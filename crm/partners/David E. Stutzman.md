## Who / why
**David E. Stutzman** — Partner, Private Clients/Trusts and Estates Group, **Seward & Kissel LLP**, New York. Practicing since 1988, he has spent over three decades on complex estate, trust and charitable planning and administration for high-net-worth individuals and families, with a client base that spans fund managers, executives and family-business owners as well as inheritors, art collectors, musicians and sports figures — he regularly advises artists and those with significant art collections specifically. He co-authored the Bloomberg BNA Tax Management Portfolio *Planning for Authors, Musicians, Artists, and Collectors*, an interdisciplinary treatise on representing creators and collectors, and won Bloomberg Tax's 2018 Outstanding Portfolio Author of the Year award for it. He is a Fellow of the American College of Trust and Estate Counsel (ACTEC), serving on its Estate and Gift Taxation Committee, a past Chair (2019-2022) of the NYC Bar Association's Estate and Gift Taxation Committee, and an affiliate member of the NYC Bar's Art Law Committee. In 2026 he was featured on an ACTEC Foundation podcast on asset protection for art across the acquisition-to-disposition lifecycle. **Public-source lead — no contact yet.**
