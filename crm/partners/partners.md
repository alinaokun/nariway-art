# Partners — referral & connection network (guide)

*Records are the individual notes in this folder; **open `partners.base`** for the sortable/filterable view. Each contact note carries an **Interactions log** — jot the date, what you discussed, and the next step every time you connect, so future-you knows exactly where you left off. Fed by [[nariway-signals]]. Professional / public info only.*

## The CRM habit (this is the "notes when I reach out" feature)
Each contact is a note with:
- **Frontmatter:** `category` · `stage` (not-contacted / contacted / met / warm) · `last_contacted` · `next_followup` · `priority` · `links`
- **## Interactions** — append a dated line every touch, e.g. `- 2026-09-03 — called re succession services; she'll intro me to a family-office GC; follow up in 2 weeks.`
- **## Notes** — background, how you know them, what to remember.
- Keep `last_contacted` / `next_followup` current so the base can show who's **due**. *(Same pattern works for a [[prospects|prospect]] once you start contacting them.)*

## Referral channels, by priority (from the founding brief)
*Referral-distributed business — ~20–30 productive relationships beat hundreds of cold prospects.*

| Channel | Value | Why | Doubles as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trusts & estates attorneys | Very high | Encounter collection succession at the moment complexity hits | discovery conversation |
| Multi-family offices | Very high | Natural users of the hybrid model; one relationship → several families | discovery conversation |
| Private wealth advisors / private banks (no art team) | Very high | Own the client, don't want every specialist | discovery conversation |
| CPAs / private-client tax partners | High | Gifts, estates, basis, charitable events reveal the problem | — |
| Fine-art insurance brokers | High | See outdated schedules, poor records | discovery conversation |
| Appraisers | High | Discover planning issues they don't want to handle | specialist bench |
| Auction-house specialists | Medium | Valuable at disposition, but transaction-motivated | — |
