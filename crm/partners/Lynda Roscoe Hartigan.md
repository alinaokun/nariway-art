## Who / why
**Lynda Roscoe Hartigan** — Margaret and Terry Stent Director, **Smithsonian American Art Museum**, effective September 8, 2026, moving from her role as Rose-Marie and Eijk van Otterloo Executive Director and CEO of the Peabody Essex Museum. Began her career at SAAM, rising to chief curator, and at PEM built major collecting and programmatic initiatives spanning photography, American art, contemporary art and global fashion. Succeeds acting director Jane Carpenter-Rock. **Public-source lead — no contact yet.**
