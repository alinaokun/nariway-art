## Who / why
**Daniel H. Weiss** — George D. Widener Director and CEO, **Philadelphia Museum of Art**, effective December 1, 2025, brought in after the board's November 2025 removal of predecessor Sasha Suda amid disclosed operating deficits and organizational turmoil. Previously President and CEO of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (2015–2023), where he was credited with steering The Met through its own financial crisis, and before that president of Haverford College and Lafayette College. Expected to lead PMA through at least 2028. **Public-source lead — no contact yet.**
