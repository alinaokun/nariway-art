# Prospects — Collection Intelligence CRM

*Collectors who may face a public-life / legacy decision. The register of the **Collection Intelligence** function, fed by [[nariway-signals]]. This is an **institutional-opportunity map, not a personal dossier.***

## Guardrail (non-negotiable — public sources only)
- Record only **publicly stated facts.** No inferred private family circumstances, health, confidential wealth estimates, home addresses, private contact details, or speculation about intentions.
- The unit is the **collection and its public signals**, not the wealthy person.
- Separate documented statements from Nariway inference. Sources: ARTnews Top 200, foundation sites & public filings, interviews, catalogues, press, loan/exhibition records.

## What counts as a signal (why a collector lands here)
Founded an art foundation · publicly discussed the collection's future or legacy · announced a major museum gift / promised gift / long-term loan · opened or announced a private exhibition space or art park · family publicly active in a collection foundation · acquired a public-facing property · began lending extensively.

## Schema
`collector · collection focus · approx. size (if public) · geography · public exposure · foundation? · current access · public legacy/succession statement · family involvement (public) · recent loans/exhibitions · pathway signal · source · date noted`

## Register → now a filterable Base
The records live as **individual notes in `crm/prospects/`** (one per collector, each with a clickable source link and a `fit` rating). **Open `prospects.base`** for the sortable/filterable table + card views — no static table to outgrow. `fit: high` = ★ best fit (living collector · distinctive collection · active legacy moment).
