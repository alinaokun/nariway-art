**Found 2026-09-11 (Nariway Signals).** Charles Asprey, of the Asprey jewellery family, gave the Pier Arts Centre in Stromness, Orkney — built on Margaret Gardiner's Modern British collection — its biggest gift since 1979: 53 contemporary works (Wolfgang Tillmans, Martin Puryear, Atelier E.B) plus funding for an artist residency and an ongoing acquisition budget. A public legacy signal from a living collector choosing to strengthen an existing small institution rather than found a new one, and explicitly framed as decentralizing collector philanthropy away from London. Not yet contacted or deep-researched; a candidate for the public-legacy-signal watchlist, not yet a warm prospect.
