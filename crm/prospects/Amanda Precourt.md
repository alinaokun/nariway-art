Denver real estate developer and collector Amanda Precourt converted a condemned fortune-cookie factory in the Baker neighborhood into Cookie Factory — a free, 5,000+ sq ft public art space with exhibition and studio space, a video/sound theater, and a sculpture garden, self-funded, opened May 2025, with Jérôme Sans as artistic director. She lives above it with her own collection. Elisa Carollo's Aug 25, 2026 Observer profile ("Collector Amanda Precourt Skipped the Vanity Museum to Give Denver a Community Art Space," flagged in Art Spell's Aug 25 roundup) frames the project explicitly against the personal-monument model — community accessibility and artist commissions over a founder's name on a building. `fit: high`: a living, culturally distinctive collector publicly narrating a legacy choice in real time, and a genuine counter-case to the mega-museum pattern (Lucas, Malba, Voorlinden) this brief tracks daily.
