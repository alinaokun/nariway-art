# Network Research — connection intelligence (guide)

A work-function (no persona) that treats Alina's existing relationships as an asset and works it deliberately. It researches people, primarily on LinkedIn, to answer one question, who in reach already sits on the path Nariway needs to travel, and what the right first move is. It feeds [[outreach]] (who to write and why) and the [[partners|partner CRM]] (who to log and nurture). It reports to the CMO lens, audience reality.

## What it does
- **Mines the existing network.** Reads Alina's LinkedIn connection exports and identifies the people who matter to Nariway, sorted into the channels below, not the whole list.
- **Researches named people.** Given a name or a target profile, assembles a short, public-source brief, who they are, why they matter to Nariway, the warm path to them, and a suggested opening.
- **Finds the gaps.** Names the referral channels the network is thin in, so networking is aimed, not random.
- **Refreshes.** A new export drops in and the function re-runs, surfacing new connections worth acting on. Light beat, surfaces through [[nariway-signals]] when there is something worth a move.

## The channels it sorts for
The relationships a legacy-of-collections advisory actually runs on.
1. **Trusts and estates attorneys** — the single most important referral partner for legacy decisions.
2. **Family offices, private banks, wealth advisors** — the trusted advisors already sitting beside the collector.
3. **Art advisors, appraisers, art-market and art-law professionals** — the specialist bench and credibility layer.
4. **Museum leadership, curators, development directors** — who signals what museums will and will not take.
5. **Philanthropy and planned-giving professionals** — the mechanics of the gift.
6. **Fine-art insurers** — early sightlines on significant collections.
7. **Collectors themselves** — rarely legible from a title, surfaced by other signals.

## How it works
- Export lives outside the vault (it holds third parties' contact details). The function reads it in place, writes only the curated, public, professional facts into the vault, name, public title, company, why-relevant, suggested move. No emails, no phone numbers, no private data committed to git.
- Current live deliverable → [[warm-network]], the ranked shortlist from the 12 Aug 2026 export.

## The privacy line
Public professional information about Alina's own connections, used for Alina's own business. The function does not compile personal, private, financial, or health data on anyone, does not scrape beyond what a connection has chosen to publish, and never puts a third party's contact details into version control. Outreach is always Alina's send, never automated.

## Standing method note
Titles come from the person's own LinkedIn profile as of the export date and may be stale. Confirm the current role before a first contact leans on it.
