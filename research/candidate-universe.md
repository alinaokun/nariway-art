# Nariway Candidate Universe — v0.1 (2026-08)

*Identify-level (not deep-research). Merged and deduped from two sweeps + the [landscape-sweep](landscape-sweep.md) 12. US-weighted with international benchmarks; emphasis modern/small-regional, classic anchors capped. This is the pool from which the **designed sample** (bottom) is drawn. Dimensions tracked: pathway · founder living/deceased · geography · collection thesis · outcome · scale.*

> **Verification status — read before this feeds any conclusion.** Every row is **Provisional** (a research *lead*) until checked against primary sources. Specific outcome labels, distress causes, sizes, and interpretive phrases (e.g. "land outran endowment") are especially to be treated as leads, not facts. **✓ = spot-verified** against a primary/authoritative source. Verified so far: Neue Galerie–Met merger (announced May 2026); Fisher→SFMOMA 100-year loan.

## Designed first 10 (draw for the sample)

Selected to **maximize variation** across pathway × founder-status × geography × thesis × outcome × scale, weighted toward the north star (distinctiveness / public-benefit) and deliberately including failures.

| # | Case | Pathway | Founder | Outcome | Verify | Why in the sample (dimension it uniquely covers) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **McNay** | Built house→museum | Dec. | Thriving | Provisional | Healthy survived-founder baseline; tests governance/money/succession/friction columns cleanly |
| 2 | **di Rosa** | Built + sculpture park | Dec. | **Distressed** | Provisional | Durability *strain* — regional thesis, campus reportedly for sale |
| 3 | **Terra Museum** | Single-collector museum → dissolved | Dec. | **Collapsed** | Provisional | A *clean institutional failure* — board/widow legal war → closed 2004 → became a grantmaking foundation; core works to the Art Institute |
| 4 | **Souls Grown Deep** | Intentional dispersal | Dec. | Thriving | Provisional | The anti-monument; overlooked community; distinctiveness *is* the thesis (north star) |
| 5 | **Kinsey Collection** | Traveling, no building | Living | Thriving | Provisional | Public life at scale without a home; audience/public-value dimension |
| 6 | **Longleaf / Berkowitz** | Abandoned plan → art park | Living | Emerging | Provisional | Changed plan + delegated stewardship; tests Decision-Owner & Friction (why abandoned) |
| 7 | **Onera Foundation** | Seasonal exhibition foundation | Living | Emerging | Provisional | Small novel-access model; access-to-the-inaccessible |

**Coverage:** now spans **success · distress · collapse · dispersal · mobility · pivot · small-access · partnership · merger · new construction.** 6 living / 4 deceased founders · geographies TX·CA·IL·GA·FL·CT·LA·NY·BC · theses span region/community/diplomacy/identity · scale from a seasonal room to 217 acres. Classic anchors (Barnes, Gardner, Menil, Shelburne) held in the universe, not the first sample.

*Change from prior draft: dropped **Thoma** (loans-only was already covered by Souls Grown Deep / Kinsey / Onera as no-building models) and added **Terra** for a true dissolution. **Corcoran** is the equally strong alternative collapse case if a larger, civic-scale failure is preferred over a single-collector one.*

**Other swaps still on the table:** *Vogel* (modest-means dispersal — challenges the "rich people" frame) · *Wolfsonian-FIU* or *Anderson@Stanford* for the university slot · *Rennie* for appointment-only · *Pyramid Hill* for a survived-founder art park.

**Recommended case #1: McNay** — completed pathway (survived founder, 1950), exercises the most schema columns at once, a *healthy* baseline to calibrate the protocol before messier cases, and the one you already have a personal read on.
