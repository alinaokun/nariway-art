# Public IA stress-test — ChatGPT's 5-territory model (2026-08-23)

> Adversarial test of the proposed reader-facing architecture (macro "Landscape" layer + 5 territories: The Collection · Ownership & Stewardship · Value & Economics · Succession · The Collection's Future; professional ecosystem distributed + one reference) against the nine-domain [[landscape-domain-audit]]. Failures first; verdict last. Per instruction: NO new categories proposed for neatness.

## Failures / exceptions (fix these)
1. **Tax mis-homed (sharpest).** Only estate & gift tax is succession. Capital-gains/collectibles + sales/use tax attach to SELLING; charitable-deduction rules attach to GIVING; international income tax to cross-border holding. Putting all Tax in Succession misleads the reader researching a sale or a gift. → Treat **Tax as a cross-cutting dimension** surfaced within each subject; feature estate/gift in Succession; maybe one tax-overview reference. (Test #7 = NO.)
2. **Cross-border / jurisdiction scattered** across T2 (jurisdiction), T4 (international inheritance), Tax. The multi-jurisdictional collector (the core audience) has no single home. → Treat **cross-border as a cross-cutting dimension**, not a T2 subject.
3. **Ownership structures / foundations / trusts triple-homed & mis-anchored** in T2. Chosen for succession/tax/going-public reasons, not daily ownership; a foundation is also a T5 destination. → Canonical home in **Succession/Future**, cross-linked; not primarily T2.
4. **Governance & durability split T4/T5** ("governance" in Succession, "durability" in Future). → One canonical home (**T5 institutional durability**), cross-link from Succession.
5. **Sale split T3 (mechanics) / T5 (decision)** — defensible but a navigation seam; must be cross-linked or it strands the reader.
6. **"The Landscape" naming collision + not a pure layer.** It's both the section title and the macro layer. Its contents (wealth transfer, private-museum landscape, market dynamics) are page-worthy subjects readers will click into, so it behaves like a 6th territory. → Rename the layer OR accept it as a light peer distinct from the section title; give the macro subjects real homes. (Test #6 = partial.)
7. **Rights mis-homed in T1** (connoisseurial). Rights are legal + revenue → fits **T2**.
8. **T5 is a mega-territory** (frame + 6 pathways + public-access + institutions + durability). Needs internal structure or it recreates the "one big section" problem.

## Nothing genuinely homeless — problems are multi-home / misleading placement, not gaps.

## What it gets right (the important part)
**Leads with "The Collection"** — the object/knowledge front-end (identity, provenance, authenticity, condition) that is Nariway's biggest evidence hole. This deliberately **corrects the searchlight bias**: the IA follows the reader's mental model ("what do I own") not Nariway's research strengths. Consequence to plan for: **T1 launches nearly empty** — use honest "in development" states, don't pad. The pathways are correctly demoted to DECISIONS inside T5, not the domain.

## Tests
- #6 Landscape as cross-cutting layer: **partial** (behaves like a light 6th territory; naming collision).
- #7 Tax primarily in Succession: **no** (cross-cutting).
- #8 The Future holds retain/sale/public-life: **mostly yes** (good that *retain* is included; sale split; territory overloaded).

## Verdict
**Survives as a reader-facing IA, with placement fixes — and improves on the four-family map by leading with the reader's real starting point.** The failures are two cross-cutting DIMENSIONS forced into single territories (Tax, Cross-border) and three HINGE subjects needing canonical homes + cross-links (structures/foundations, governance/durability, sale), plus the Landscape naming. Fixes are dimensions + cross-links, not new territories. The five territories are the right five.
