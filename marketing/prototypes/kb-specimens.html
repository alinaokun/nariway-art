A collection transfers. Everything around it may not.

What transfers

  • Legal ownership of the works
  • The objects themselves

What may not

  • The knowledge of what each work is and why it was acquired
  • The relationships with artists, dealers and institutions
  • The money and effort needed to store, insure and care for it
  • The collector's intent for what it should become
