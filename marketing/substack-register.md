# Substack Register — whom to read, engage, and follow

Built by the [[substack]] function. The Substack and adjacent writers relevant to Nariway's world, sorted into three tiers. **Read** are the authorities to know closely (feeds [[learning|Learning]]). **Engage** are peers where a genuine restack or comment builds Alina's presence and audience, since on Substack that engagement, plus mutual recommendations, is the main organic growth channel (feeds the CMO and [[network-research|Network Research]]). **Follow** is a lighter watch.

**Verification note, in force.** Some entries below were confirmed on their about page; others were seen in search results only and are marked *confirm on first visit*. Do not cite or publicly recommend a flagged one until it is opened and confirmed, same discipline as the [[claims-register]] and the reading list.

**How to use it.** Concentrate. A few deep, recurring engagements with the Engage tier beat broad low-effort likes. Comment substantively on the Engage writers, set up mutual Recommend relationships with the on-audience peers, and restack with a real one-line take rather than a bare restack. Prioritize anyone writing on collecting, collection legacy, and private-to-public transitions.

## Read — authorities to know closely

**[The Gray Market](https://thegraymarket.substack.com)** — Tim Schneider (former Artnet art-business editor). Free plus paid, high cadence. The most rigorous independent analyst of art-market mechanics on Substack, markets, law, finance, institutions. Essential background for any argument about how value and money move around a collection. *Also an Engage target, a restack here reaches exactly the right readership.* **Status: subscribed (Aug 2026).**

**[The Art Bystander](https://theartbystander.substack.com)** — Roland-Philippe Kretzschmar. Free plus paid, high cadence, large cross-platform following. On the shifting power structures of the art world with a strong collector-facing lens, directly on Nariway's terrain of taste, collector behavior, and institutional power. Its Aug 2026 wealth-transfer analysis is logged in [[market-intelligence]] #1. *Read plus Engage.* **Status: trialing, 7-day (Aug 2026).**

**[Provenance Watch](https://provenancewatch.substack.com)** — a provenance researcher and activist. Free plus paid, one to two posts a week. Cultural property, provenance, restitution, the title and provenance risk that shadows any major collection entering an institution. The art-law and cultural-property brief.

### Authorities to read who are not on Substack (track at source)
- **Katya Kazakina**, "The Art Detective," weekly market column at [Artnet Pro](https://news.artnet.com). Already in Alina's orbit. No Substack.
- **Georgina Adam**, "Art Market Eye," [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com). The senior authority on art-market structure. No Substack.
- **Marion Maneker**, now "Wall Power" at [Puck](https://puck.news). His old Artelligence Substack is effectively dormant, follow him at Puck.
- **Josh Baer**, [The Baer Faxt](https://thebaerfaxt.com), insider market intelligence since 1994. Own site and podcast, not Substack.
- **[Center for Art Law](https://itsartlaw.org)**, the reference source for deaccessioning, estate planning, and cultural-property law. Worth a standing subscription. Already on the [[learning|reading list]].

## Engage — peers where engagement builds presence

**[Less Than Half](https://lessthanhalf.substack.com)** — Hall W. Rockefeller. Women artists and women as collectors, collecting as impact, the idea of "Matronage." Already in Alina's orbit and in the [[partners]] CRM, and the closest peer on the collecting-philosophy axis. The highest-value engagement relationship on this list. *Read plus Engage.* **Status: paid subscriber.**

**[Art Forecast](https://artforecast.substack.com)** — Tatum Dooley (writes for Artforum, AD, Vogue). Free plus paid, thousands of subscribers, pivoting in 2026 toward the people who make up the art world rather than market gossip. An established essayistic peer with a sensibility close to Artobiography. A strong cross-recommendation candidate.

**[The Gallery Companion](https://www.thegallerycompanion.com)** — Dr. Victoria Powell. Accessible, essayistic writing on art's cultural significance, roughly every two to three weeks, active subscriber chat. Register matches Artobiography. *Engage or Follow.*

**[Curatorial Affairs](https://curatorialaffairs.substack.com)** — Jacob Barnes and Morgane Wagner. Serious contemporary art criticism aiming to revive real art writing, the analytical non-gossip quadrant Alina fits. *Confirm authors on first visit.*

**[Anne Parke Art Advisory](https://anneparke.substack.com)** — Anne Parke (former David Zwirner, Fergus McCaffrey). Contemporary artists worth collecting, market literacy for collectors. An advisor-peer speaking to the same audience. *Confirm on first visit.*

**[JAD Art Advisory](https://jadartadvisory.substack.com)** — Jessica Arb Danial. Advisory and collection-building, running about three years. Another collector-facing advisor peer. *Engage or Follow.*

**[Permanent Collections](https://permanentcollections.substack.com)** — Thomas Brown. Essays on individual museums' permanent collections, about monthly, directly on the museum-collection theme. Use this URL, not the profile handle (a different Thomas Brown writes a dating newsletter). *Engage or Follow.*

**[On Collecting](https://oncollecting.substack.com)** — Grant Pierrus Art Advisory. Collecting art and design as practice, an adjacent audience. *Confirm on first visit.*

## Follow — lighter touch, useful signal

**[Art Spell](https://www.artspell.media)** — free, near-daily roundup of art-world media across museums, galleries, heritage, and market. Efficient way to monitor the whole field, including collection and museum stories, in one place.

**[Art Every Day](https://arteveryday.substack.com)** — George Bothamley. Accessible art-history storytelling, five days a week, about 32,000 readers. Already in Alina's orbit and in the [[partners]] CRM. Not on the market or legacy beat, but a very large engaged audience, so a recommendation or occasional restack exchange is a real reach play. *Engage opportunistically for reach.*

**[ART XCHANGE](https://artxchange.substack.com)** — art-market data commentary. *Confirm cadence and author on first visit.*

**[Art Meets Culture](https://artmeetsculture.substack.com)** — practical collecting guidance for newer collectors. Lighter than Nariway's altitude, useful as a read on how emerging collectors are taught to think. *Confirm on first visit.*

## The growth mechanics (why the Engage tier matters commercially)
- **Notes is the discovery engine.** Short original Notes plus restacks with a genuine one-line take put Alina in the restacker's followers' feeds, the main source of organic reach.
- **Recommendations compound.** A mutual Recommend with an on-audience peer converts far better than cold Notes, because it fires at the reader's own subscribe moment. Target the collecting and essayistic peers above.
- **Concentrate, do not spray.** A few deep recurring engagements with writers whose readers are collectors beat broad shallow activity.
