## What makes a Note perform now (2026)
- **Restacks are the currency.** The algorithm weights restacks above everything; one restack from a larger account can out-reach fifty of your own Notes. High-signal *replies* now beat "love this" reactions.
- **The first ~3 lines decide everything.** Open with a specific claim, image, or curiosity gap that stops the scroll.
- **Short, plain, read-aloud.** Write like texting a friend. Whitespace, no filler. (This is also the [[ai-tells]] discipline.)
- **Add an image.** ~1 in 3 Notes now carries a photo; text-only leaves reach on the table. For an art publication this is a structural edge, images for everything.
- **Emotional recognition beats cleverness.** Aim for "oh, me too," not a smart line.

## What changed recently (why this matters)
- **Late-2025 shift:** the feed stopped prioritizing people you follow, so most of what any reader sees is from accounts they have never followed. Notes are now a genuine **discovery** surface, not a broadcast.
- **The "Notes bubble" burst.** Chasing virality now builds an email list, not engaged readers. Sarah Fay's data: a Note with 85 likes converted the same ~100 subscribers as one with 12,000 likes. **Going viral usually does not lead to engaged subscribers.**
- **Re-share your own strong Note ~24h later** for a documented 10–20% lift.

## Cadence and conversion
- **Notes get you found; posts make people stay.** Followers are not subscribers; conversion happens when a Note earns enough trust to click through.
- **Participate, don't broadcast** (Fay's "dinner party"): boosting other writers and adding real replies in active comment sections is the single most effective growth behavior.
- **Direct outreach** to engaged readers reportedly converts well.
- **Cadence:** 1 Note/day keeps you visible; 2–3/day is the growth sweet spot; above ~5 fatigues people. Consistency over volume.

## Mistakes that kill Notes
Treating Notes as an afterthought · not replying to comments · AI-sounding text (increasingly recognized) · restacking with no added comment · over-polishing before posting.

## The "quote your own post" feature — yes, use it
It exists and is current. Two mechanisms:
1. **Restack a post** — sends the whole post into the feed as a Note; add your own line on top.
2. **Restack quote / quote card** — highlight any passage while reading, click **Restack quote**, and it becomes a **visual quote card** (now treated like an image in the feed, with customizable background color and alignment, and room for your text above). [On Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/new-read-aloud-voices-quote-restacks)
**Verdict: worth using, and underused rather than underperforming.** A quote card satisfies the "include an image" rule automatically and rides the top-weighted restack signal (one source cites +30% views). The catch: a *bare* card with no commentary underperforms; its value is the line you pick plus what you say around it. **Ideal for Artobiography**, because her essays are full of clean, self-contained sentences that stand alone. Note: restacks and quote cards appear in Notes only, they are not emailed to subscribers.
