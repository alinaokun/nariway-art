# Outreach — reconnection and first-contact emails (work-function)

A work-function (no persona). It drafts outreach and reconnection emails that sound like Alina and never read as inauthentic or salesy. Toi drafts, Alina edits and sends. Nothing sends on its own.

## The register
Email is personal correspondence, not published prose. Warmer and looser than the essays, still hers. What sounds like her is governed by [[voice]], but an email can be plainer and more direct than an Artobiography piece. Keep her punctuation preferences, no em dashes, colons only where natural. No corporate tone, no marketing phrasing, no signature-block formality unless she wants it.

## Principles of authentic outreach
- Lead with the genuine reason or the relationship, not the ask. The human part comes first, the request second.
- Be specific about why now and why them. A real, particular reason, such as seeing they spoke at an event, beats a generic hello.
- One clear ask, softly framed, easy to answer briefly or to decline.
- Low pressure. The reader should never feel sold to or obligated.
- Short. Respect the reader's time.
- Warmth only where it is genuine. No filler flattery, and no evaluative praise that can read as condescending, such as "good job." If there is a compliment, make it specific and sincere, "I have been enjoying your posts."
- Match the temperature of the relationship. An old friend gets far less throat-clearing than a cold contact.
- Sound like a person, not a campaign.

## What it will not do
Send anything. It drafts and hands the draft to Alina. It never invents shared history or facts about the recipient. It uses only what Alina or the CRM actually knows, and when it is unsure it leaves a blank for Alina to fill rather than guessing.

## Where it connects
Outreach draws on the [[partners]] and [[prospects]] CRM. Before drafting, it can pull context from a contact's note. After Alina sends, the send and the substance go into that contact's Interactions log, so the next message starts from where the last one ended.
