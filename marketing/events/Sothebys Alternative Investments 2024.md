**Five-day course on Alternative Investments (art as an asset class), taken by Alina in 2024.** She rated it **one of the best courses she has ever taken**, which makes Sotheby's Institute short courses a proven-quality source for her (see [[events-radar]] and [[learning]]).

**Why it matters beyond the course, the pivot it caused.** Alina took it early in Artobiography, when she expected to write about art as an investment class. The course was excellent, and it convinced her of the opposite: the art market is so opaque, and price formation so arbitrary, that she did not want to build around art investing. That is when she turned to writing about artists, and later to Nariway's current work on the future of significant collections. So her rejection of the art-as-investment frame is informed and firsthand, not a hunch, which reinforces [[positioning]]: Nariway is about legacy and stewardship, not art as a financial instrument.
