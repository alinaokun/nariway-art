Aspen Institute's Artist-Endowed Foundations Initiative runs a small, competitive-application seminar for people who lead or govern artist-endowed foundations, foundations built directly from an artist's own estate and holdings. The 2026 cohort (26 people) just completed; the next runs spring 2027. Unlike a conference, this is a collegial working cohort of people who have personally converted a private estate/collection into a governed foundation and now run it, the closest thing to a peer-learning room for the exact transition Nariway studies, on the "artist's own foundation" branch specifically rather than the collector-museum branch. Surfaced 2026-08-17 by the network-research scan; worth deciding on ahead of the 2027 application window.
