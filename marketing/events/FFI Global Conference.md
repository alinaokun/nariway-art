Family Firm Institute's annual gathering of family-enterprise advisors worldwide, attorneys, wealth advisors, and, distinctively, family-dynamics/psychology specialists who serve multi-generational family enterprises. Open registration, workshop scale, not a trade show. Answers: how professionals who specialize in the *psychological and governance* side of family transitions (not just legal/tax mechanics) see legacy-asset decisions unfold and where they refer clients — a room STEP, ACTEC, and Purposeful Planning don't quite cover. Home-turf NYC, early-fall.
