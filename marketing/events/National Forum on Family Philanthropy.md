National Center for Family Philanthropy's annual forum: philanthropic families and their advisors, peer-networking plenary and breakout format. Answers the "donate" branch of the keep/divide/lend/donate/sell decision specifically at the family level (private foundations, donor structures), more family-specific than the generic Council on Foundations conference (checked and excluded, broad nonprofit-sector advocacy with no succession angle). Drivable from Montvale. **Note:** dates overlap AAA National Conference (Nov 5-6, NYC) — both are home-turf/no-lodging, so a tight two-city week is feasible but check before committing to both.
