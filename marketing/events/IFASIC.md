International Fine Art and Specie Insurance Conference, a not-for-profit industry conference founded 2009, one of the largest fine-art-and-specie insurance educational gatherings in the market. Rotates internationally (Antwerp, London, New York historically; Athens 2025; Marrakech Oct 21-23, 2026). Draws a genuine cross-section of the risk side of the art world: underwriters, brokers, claims adjusters, loss adjusters, lawyers, forensic accountants, art appraisers, private investigators, and law-enforcement/diamond-certification experts — 150+ organizations from 25+ countries, junior staff through group CEOs. 2026 program includes museum thefts, conservation tools/technology, safety-deposit-box risk, and valuing coins/watches/gold alongside fine art. Answers: what actually triggers a claim or loss event on a collection (theft, conservation failure, storage/transit risk) and how the insurance side of the market — not the tax or estate side — sees a collection's vulnerabilities; a more established, international counterpart to the newer St. John's "Art at Risk" thread already tracked ([[St Johns Art at Risk Conference]]). Open registration, no membership gate, but genuinely industry-facing rather than collector-facing — worth scouting the program once published rather than assuming fit. Geography is the real cost here: Marrakech in 2026 is a fly-and-stay trip, not a drive; worth re-checking when a future edition lands in New York, its historical home base.
