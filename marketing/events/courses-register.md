# Courses Register — programs worth attending

Maintained by the events-research function (see [[events-radar]]), cross-linked to [[learning]]. Professional short and executive courses relevant to Nariway, judged on two axes: **information** (authority in the field) and **connections** (the caliber of the cohort). Verified against provider pages in August 2026. Course dates, prices, and formats change yearly, so treat specifics as indicative and confirm on the linked page before committing. When Alina decides to pursue one, it gets promoted to an event note (`category: course`) so it shows in `events.base` with a decision and cost.

**The two priorities for Alina's exact position** are marked below.

---

## Sotheby's Institute of Art
Proven quality for Alina (she rated their [Alternative Investments course](Sothebys%20Alternative%20Investments%202024.md) among the best she has taken). Short courses run one day to twelve weeks across New York, London, and online. Hub: [short courses](https://sothebysinstitute.com/continuing-education/short-courses/) · [online](https://sothebysinstitute.com/continuing-education/online-courses/).

- **PRIORITY — Art, Finance, and Investment, 5-day intensive (New York).** BOTH. ~$2,500 ($2,300 early; rises after Sep 1, 2026). Fall Oct 26 to 30, 2026; Spring Jan 25 to 29, 2027. Instructor Andrea Danese (ex-CEO, Athena Art Finance). The natural successor to the course she loved, art as an asset class, art-backed lending, funds, tokenization, and the financial mechanics behind how large collections are valued, transferred, and monetized. In-person NY cohort of advisors and collectors. [Link](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/5-day-intensive/new-york-art-finance-and-investment/)
- **Art Law, live online (taught by [[Judith Prowda]]).** BOTH. ~$1,590 (comparable Institute online pricing; dedicated page would not load, confirm). A two-birds option, real art-law content (artist-dealer relations, private sales, restitution, authenticity disputes, all live when a collection is gifted, loaned, or dispersed) and a natural reconnection with Judith, whom Alina already knows. Confirm format, dates, price via the online hub or professional@sia.edu.
- **Collecting, online, 6 weeks.** INFORMATION. ~$1,590. Instructor Betsy Thomas (collection development and estate management). On collection-building, due diligence, authenticity, and inventory and estate management, the stewardship side of Nariway. [Link](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/online-courses/collecting/)
- **Introduction to Art Business, 5-day (NY).** Leans CONNECTIONS (she has the information). Fall Oct 5 to 9, 2026; Spring Apr 19 to 23, 2027. [Link](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/5-day-intensive/new-york-introduction-to-art-business/)
- **Art Value and Investment, online, 6 weeks.** INFORMATION. Overlaps the finance course. [Link](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/online-courses/art-value-and-investment/)
- Lower priority, verified to exist: [Introduction to Art and Finance (online)](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/online-courses/introduction-to-art-and-finance/); [Art and Finance in a Global Market (NY evening)](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/evening-courses/new-york-art-and-finance-in-a-global-market/); [Collecting Photography (5-day NY)](https://sothebysinstitute.com/courses/5-day-intensive/collecting-photography/).

## UC Berkeley Law — Art, Law, and Finance Certificate
- **Strong on paper, but ~$5,000 tuition plus Bay Area travel and lodging (~$6.5–7k all-in) for four days. Decision 2026-08: defer.** BOTH. Four days, week of Nov 30, 2026, at UC Berkeley Law. Cohort capped at ~30, a mixed room of collectors, advisors, museum professionals, and attorneys on exactly the legal-financial forces that govern a collection entering public life. The connections are real, but that is a large spend before any revenue, and firsthand research (the near-free [[regional-visits]]) plus the market conversations give more authority per dollar right now. Revisit when there is revenue or a specific reason. Confirm tuition at artlaw@berkeley.edu. Companion symposium at SFMOMA and an intensive seminar under the same project are lower-commitment touchpoints. [Program](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/research/business/art-law-finance-project/2026-berkeley-art-law-and-finance-certificate-program/) · [hub](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/research/business/art-law-finance-project/)

## Christie's Education
Comparable authority to Sotheby's Institute, with auction-house access. **Caveat: the christies.edu site would not load during research, so current 2026 titles, dates, and fees are unverified, confirm on the live site.**
- **Short Courses (New York)** — historically evening or short sessions for collectors and professionals, a good connections vehicle. [Link](https://christies.edu/new-york/courses/continuing-education/short-course)
- **Art Business Certificate** — year-long (about September to April), five modules of weekly evening sessions, in NY and London in parallel. Deeper than a short course, modular.

## Center for Art Law
BOTH, low cost of entry. Brooklyn nonprofit running virtual and in-person conferences, lectures, workshops, and online courses at the art-law intersection, provenance, restitution, authenticity, cultural heritage. The connective tissue of the US art-law community, and already on the [[learning|reading list]]. [Online courses](https://itsartlaw.org/courses-and-programs/online-courses/) · [worldwide course directory](https://itsartlaw.org/art-law-courses-and-programs-worldwide/)

## The Attingham Trust — Summer School
CONNECTIONS, elite but a stretch on eligibility. A 16-day residential course touring British country houses and collections (2026: Jun 27 to Jul 12), the gold-standard cohort for museum and heritage collections professionals. Pitched at curators, conservators, and archivists with prior grounding, so fit depends on how Alina frames candidacy. Application deadline Jan 30, 2026. [UK](https://www.attinghamtrust.org/) · [US applicants](https://americanfriendsofattingham.org/courses/summer-school-2026/)
