## Evidence-phase rooms — the practitioner ecosystem (priority now)
The goal now is not to find collectors (hard to reach, rarely ready to discuss something this personal) but the **ecosystem that has already witnessed these decisions**: one good trusts-and-estates attorney has seen ~30 collection transitions, a family-office exec ~10, an art insurer ~50. Efficient teachers. **Discipline (from the Network-Intelligence brief): recommend a room only if it puts experienced practitioners in real interaction AND answers a specific [[problem-discovery|discovery]] question, never just because it concerns art or wealth.** For each: which question it answers, who is there, access, cost, why it is worth the founder's time.
