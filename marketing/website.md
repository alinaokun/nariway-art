**Before finalizing:**
1. **Fisher number — RESOLVED by deep research (2026-08-14).** The concept's "more than 1,000 works" is wrong. Per **SFMOMA's own press release (primary)**, the 2009 agreement placed **"more than 720 works by 100 artists"** on long-term loan (term: initially 25 years, extended to 100, renewable in 25-year increments). The ~1,100 figure is the *total* collection (secondary), not the loan. **Final homepage copy:** "Instead they reached a long-term partnership with SFMOMA, placing **more than 720 works by 100 artists** there under a 100-year loan." Source: [SFMOMA press release](https://www.sfmoma.org/press-release/sfmoma-to-unveil-major-transformation-of-the-renowned-doris-and-donald-fisher-collection-the-first-full-reinstallation-of-the-collection-at-sfmoma-since-2016/). Our coded [[fisher-sfmoma]] already had this right (720+/100).
2. **Advisory line, optional soften (her call).** "ADVISORY — We work with collectors and families facing those decisions themselves" still reads as an active practice with clients; better placed now (inside "What Nariway does") and the CONTACT is honest. Willingness-to-pay is untested, so if she wants to stay honest about stage: "Independent guidance for a collector or family through the decision itself, owing nothing to a sale or an institutional outcome." Keeps the key differentiator without implying a current roster.
3. **Minor:** the pathways block is fragment-heavy (fine as punchy design copy); the founder line dropped the memorable "as much as taste" (the new "capital, governance, risk, institutions, and long-term durability" is good and more complete, just a trade-off); confirm GET IN TOUCH and READ ARTOBIOGRAPHY resolve to a real contact and the Substack.
