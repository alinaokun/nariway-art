## Auction previews (free, rare public viewings of great collections)
Sotheby's and Christie's open their sale lots to the public for several days before an auction, a rare chance to see privately held works up close. Alina wants alerts to significant single-collection sales and their preview windows.
- **Christie's, the Alan Medaugh collection of Hiroshige.** 600+ Japanese woodblock prints, one of the finest Hiroshige collections ever assembled. **First sale Sept 15, 2026** (180+ prints; more sessions to come, undated), at **Christie's, 20 Rockefeller Plaza, NY**. The public preview is typically the ~4–5 days before, so roughly **Sept 10–14**, but Christie's has not published it. **Action for Alina: confirm the preview dates at [christies.com](https://www.christies.com/en/auctions) or call the Asian Art department, and go.** Also a report case, see [[candidate-universe]]. [ARTnews](https://www.artnews.com/art-news/news/christies-alan-medaugh-hiroshige-collection-auction-1234794665/)
- **New York marquee auction week, ~mid-November 2026** (Christie's, Sotheby's, Phillips). Single-owner collections are almost always featured, with free public previews the ~4–7 days before the evening sales. Fall lineups and preview dates are usually announced early-to-mid October; Signals will watch the house calendars and flag anything worth seeing.
