# Art I've Seen — Alina's private art record

A running record of works Alina has personally encountered and wanted to remember. **Rule: save only what you want to remember.** Some visits produce three works, some twelve, some none. Not a blog, not an obligation.

A curated subset of these can later become a visual **"Art I've Seen"** page on alinaokun.com (a grid of works, each with a line on why it was saved, filterable by artist / museum / city / year). Publish later, or never. This file is the private source; the public page would be a chosen subset.

**Two layers per entry.**
- *Public (if ever published):* the work, the artist, where and when Alina saw it, and one or two lines on why she saved it.
- *Private refresher (never published):* pronunciation, the artist's dates/nationality, where Alina first saw the artist, other works she has seen, one memorable fact. This is what powers the quick "who might be there" note before an event, so names reconnect to things Alina actually experienced, rather than being memorized cold.
