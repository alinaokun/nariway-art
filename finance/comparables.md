# Comparables — businesses worth studying (CFO)

A CFO study ledger. Adjacent businesses Nariway can learn from, how they package, price, and build revenue, without copying them. The point is ideas and cautions, not imitation. A real business teaches more than a whiteboard. Each entry: what it is, how it makes money, how it differs from Nariway, what is transferable, and what to avoid.

## Nina Surtees — smART Advisory
*nycgallerina.com · added 2026-08-12 at Alina's request.*

Alina's read: interesting, but a different business and a different background from hers. The CFO agrees, and studies it for ideas rather than as a model to follow. (Correcting an earlier overstatement, this was once called Alina's "closest peer" in [[warm-network]]; it is not.)

**What it is.** An art advisory with three lines: a **smART Collectors Club** (membership, curated works, workshops, events), **1:1 art advising** (acquisition, collection management, some legacy and catalogue raisonné work), and **resources for artists** (webinars, career and legacy documentation). Tagline: "Your Personal Guide to Creating and Curating an Artful Life and Legacy." Positioned around contemporary art, Web3, artist development, and "beyond the white cube," with art framed as lifestyle rather than financial investment. Founder background is curatorial and art-historical.

**How it makes money.** Recurring membership fees, hourly or project-based advising, and future paid workshops. A ladder from low commitment (club) to high (1:1).

**How it differs from Nariway.** She helps people *build and curate* collections and helps *artists* develop and document careers, for emerging and lifestyle collectors. Nariway advises *significant* collectors on what becomes of an *existing* major collection, its passage into public life. Her legacy is an artist's impact and a well-curated life. Nariway's legacy is a strategic decision with institutional stakes. Different clients, different problem, different founder background (curator and Web3 versus Alina's finance and strategy).

**What is transferable (the ideas).**
1. **A recurring, productized leg alongside 1:1.** The Collectors Club is recurring revenue, community, and lead generation at once. That is precisely the shape the CFO's million-dollar math needs, since bespoke advising alone caps around 300 to 700k ([[business-model-ledger]]). The Nariway analog is not a buy-art club, it is something like a paid briefing or benchmarking product, or a small legacy-planning circle, for collectors or their advisors.
2. **A tiered offering ladder** so prospects self-select upward from low commitment to high.
3. **Media credibility as a growth engine.** She leverages press. Nariway's version is Artobiography and the authority-building in [[learning]].

**What to avoid.** Do not drift into acquisition advising, artist services, or lifestyle curation. That collapses Nariway into the crowded "art advisor" category and blurs the high-end, disposition-strategy positioning ([[positioning]]). Nina is a useful reminder of the category Nariway is deliberately not in.

**Open question for H7B.** Does a recurring or productized leg actually command willingness to pay among *significant* collectors, or only in the emerging and lifestyle segment Nina serves? Untested. Flag for a later bounded offer.
