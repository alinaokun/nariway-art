# Nariway — Problem Discovery (market conversations)

*Owned by the [[nariway-cmo|CMO]] for framing/targets and the [[nariway-cfo|CFO]] for the evidence (H7A). A **standalone research activity — not marketing, not gated on any article, no pitch, no price-test.** Truthful, because you are genuinely doing the research.*

## The ask (no article, no pitch)
> I'm researching what happens to significant private art collections when collectors begin thinking about succession, public access, or legacy. I'm trying to understand how these decisions are handled today and where the process gets difficult. Would you be willing to talk for 20 minutes?

## Who (~5 to start — begin with people you already know)
Estate / private-client attorneys · multi-family-office executives · fine-art insurers · (private bankers).

## What to learn (moves H7A — demand side)
- When a collector starts thinking about a collection's future, who is involved first?
- What decisions are hardest? Where does the process break down?
- What outcomes do collectors actually consider?
- What's handled well today, and what isn't?
- Who has authority to hire an outside advisor — and when would one be useful?
- What do they pay for adjacent work?

## Rules
- No article. No pitch. **No price-testing** (that's H7B, and it comes later, only after a recurring problem is confirmed).
- Do **not** import the McNay story — market discovery ≠ article distribution.
- Log what you hear; route insights to the CFO's H7A evidence.
