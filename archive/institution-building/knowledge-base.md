# Institution-Building Knowledge Base

*Thin-but-reliable, per [[institution-building]]. Organized by **expertise domain** (the [[decision-map]] is organized by lifecycle stage; the two connect via the "appears in" column). **Not encyclopedic** — depth lives in the specialists ([[specialist-map]]); Nariway holds enough to frame, sequence, and challenge. Seeded only from what we already know; the rest accretes from cases + conversations. Quantitative claims are **referenced from the [[claims-register]]**, not restated here.*

## Cross-cutting
- **Feasibility economics** [DOC]: comparable services price at concept/master-planning **$30–100K+**; feasibility studies **~0.1–0.5% of project capital**. (See [[claims-register]].)
- **AAM accreditation** [DOC]: ≥2 yrs open, ≥1,000 public hrs/yr, ≥1 paid museum professional, 80% accessioned; 8–16 month process. A late-stage goal, not day one.
- **Public benefit** runs through *every* domain — it is not a single row; it is the reason the institution exists (see the *public case* gate in [[decision-map]]).
