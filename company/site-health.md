## What it can and cannot see
- **Can (no credentials):** indexability (robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical, noindex, HTTPS, redirects), title tags and meta descriptions, Open Graph / Twitter cards (link previews), heading structure and alt text, schema.org structured data, obvious performance red flags, broken links, and whether Google has indexed the pages (via a site: search).
- **Cannot (needs a connection):** real traffic and visitor behavior (Vercel Analytics / Google Analytics) and Search Console data (impressions, clicks, search queries, coverage errors). Those live behind Alina's accounts. See the connection checklist in [[website]] / below; once a connector is authorized, a separate step pulls the numbers into the check-in.
