# Vault review (12 August 2026)

*A pass over everything built so far, sorted into keep, consolidated, cut, proposed, and the honest gaps. The point is to separate what makes Nariway a real company from what is just tidy noise.*

## Core, keep (the real assets)
- **company/** — identity, org, positioning, voice, advisory panel, decisions. The spine.
- **research/** — research program, case-study protocol, claims register, Signals. The intellectual engine and its guardrails.
- **cases/** — the case studies, Cases.base, candidate universe. Core IP, now all in one folder.
- **institution-building/** — decision map, knowledge base, specialist map. The advisory IP.
- **crm/** — prospects, partners. Relationships.
- **finance/** — CFO, ledgers, problem discovery.
- **marketing/** — CMO, events, LinkedIn, website, Substack, travel, experiment ledger.
- **artobiography/** — format, structure study. Editorial.

## Consolidated this pass
- Cases.base moved into `cases/` (it was loose at the root).
- candidate universe moved into `cases/` (case data belongs with cases).
- Every event note now carries a `cost`, shown in `events.base`.

## Cut (noise)
- **distribution-map-mcnay** removed. It was a premature per-piece artifact (McNay is not published) and does not scale, one per article. The pattern now lives as one line in the CMO doc: when a piece publishes, its distribution list is created then, with the piece.

## Proposed, your call
- **Fold opportunity-radar into Signals** as a fifth beat (opportunities: podcasts, guest essays, collaborations). It overlaps Signals and events and is thin as a standalone.
- **candidate-universe as a full filterable board (about 55 notes).** Reasonable question, is it worth 55 stub notes, or is the universe better as a reference list you promote from when a case enters the pipeline. My lean is to keep it a reference for now and promote a case to a note only when it goes active. Less noise. If you want the full board, I will build it.
- **Reference artifacts** (landscape sweep, editorial structure study, the inaugural CFO brief) are historical, the reasoning behind decisions already made. Keep, but they could move to an archive area later. Low priority.

## The honest gap
Not more machinery. The vault is unusually complete for a company with no revenue. The genuine gaps are not documents.
1. A first real market conversation.
2. A first published Artobiography piece.
3. A first client.
Everything built serves those three, and adding more internal structure now would be the actual noise. The one light document worth adding later is a simple roadmap tying the pieces to milestones (launch, first conversation, first engagement, the path to a million), but only if it drives action rather than decorates.
When there is a client: entity, engagement letter and scope templates, and a deal pipeline. Not before.

## Single sources of truth (reference, do not restate)
To stop the same information from living in several tables worded slightly differently, each kind of canonical information has one home, and other pages link to it rather than repeat it. When a number or a claim changes, it changes once, in its home.
- Numbers and statistics, [[claims-register]].
- External identity and positioning, [[positioning]].
- Written voice and craft standards, [[voice]].
- Hypotheses and their status, [[research-program]].
- Growing data, the folder base for each set, Cases.base, prospects, partners, events, specialists.
Base-worthy data (grows, worth filtering) becomes a folder of notes with a base. Repeated prose or a repeated small table gets one home and a link. Genuinely small and static tables stay as tables.

## Verdict
The company is over-built on the inside and under-tested on the outside. The next unit of effort belongs outward, not in the vault.
