# Nariway Advisory Panel — how decisions get deliberated

*When Alina faces a decision worth more than a moment's thought, a real spend, a trip, a commitment, a positioning choice, she does not weigh it alone. [[chief-of-staff|Toi]] convenes the relevant lenses, each argues its perspective, Toi synthesizes with the disagreement made explicit, and **Alina decides.** A deliberation tool, not a vote.*

## The lenses
- **CFO** — economic reality. All-in cost, what it buys, right use of scarce capital and attention.
- **CMO** — audience and relationships. Does it build the right relationships and reputation, and does it fit the founder's temperament.
- **Research** — evidence and intellect. Does it advance what we actually know.
- **Founder-Life** — energy, enjoyment, fit. Does it suit Alina, and is she set up to extract its value.
- **Skeptic** — Toi assigns one lens to argue the least comfortable side, especially against an option Alina is excited about, to guard against enthusiasm bias.

*(Toi convenes only the relevant lenses, not always all five.)*

## The guardrail (same as the [[executive-model]])
These are same-substrate AI lenses, not independent minds. Convergence among them is not corroboration; it can be the same blind spot twice. They surface considerations and tensions; they never vote, never decide, never override Alina's sequencing. Alina integrates and owns the call.

## Honest note, this is not a real board
A genuine human board of advisors, a museum director, an estate attorney, a family-office principal, a fellow founder, is a separate and valuable asset, best assembled once there is a business to advise, and well sourced from the very relationships the [[nariway-signals|Signals]] and [[events-radar|events]] work is building. This panel is a thinking tool, not a substitute for real outside counsel on legal, tax, or financial specifics.

## The ritual
1. Alina names the decision, or Toi flags one.
2. Toi convenes the relevant lenses and a Skeptic.
3. Toi synthesizes into a recommendation, the tension made explicit, and the inputs still needed.
4. **Alina decides.**
5. Recorded in `company/decisions/`, question, perspectives, decision, why, and later the outcome, so we learn which advice was actually good.

## When to convene
Material or hard-to-reverse choices: real spend, travel, public commitments, positioning, engaging a specialist. Not every small thing.
