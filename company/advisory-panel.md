# Nariway Advisory Panel — how decisions get deliberated

*When Alina faces a decision worth more than a moment's thought — a real spend, a trip, a commitment, a positioning choice — she doesn't weigh it alone. [[chief-of-staff|Toi]] convenes the relevant lenses, each argues its perspective, Toi synthesizes with the disagreement made explicit, and **Alina decides.** A deliberation tool — not a vote.*

## The lenses
- **💰 CFO** — economic reality: all-in cost, what it buys, right use of scarce capital/attention?
- **🎯 CMO** — audience & relationships: does it build the right relationships/reputation? fit the founder's temperament?
- **🔬 Research** — evidence & intellect: does it advance what we actually know / the hypotheses?
- **🌱 Founder-Life** — energy, enjoyment, fit: does it suit Alina, and is she set up to extract its value?
- **😈 Skeptic** — Toi assigns one lens to argue the *least comfortable* side, especially against an option Alina is excited about (guards against enthusiasm bias).

*(Toi convenes only the relevant lenses — not always all five.)*

## The guardrail (same as the [[executive-model]])
These are same-substrate AI lenses, **not independent minds.** Convergence among them is **not** corroboration — they can share a blind spot. They surface considerations and tensions; they **never vote, never decide, never override Alina's sequencing.** Alina integrates and owns the call.

## Honest note — this is not a real board
A genuine **human board of advisors** (a museum director, an estate attorney, a family-office principal, a fellow founder) is a separate and valuable asset, best assembled once there's a business to advise — and well-sourced from the very relationships the [[nariway-signals|Signals]]/[[events-radar|events]] work is building. This panel is a *thinking tool*, not a substitute for real outside counsel on legal / tax / financial specifics.

## The ritual
1. Alina names the decision (or Toi flags one).
2. Toi convenes the relevant lenses + a Skeptic.
3. Toi synthesizes → a recommendation, the tension made explicit, and the inputs still needed.
4. **Alina decides.**
5. Recorded in `company/decisions/` — question · perspectives · decision · why · (later) **outcome**, so we learn which advice was actually good.

## When to convene
Material or hard-to-reverse choices: real spend, travel, public commitments, positioning, engaging a specialist. Not every small thing.
