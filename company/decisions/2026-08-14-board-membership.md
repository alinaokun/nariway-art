# Decision — Joining a nonprofit board (via BoardAssist)

*Raised by Alina 2026-08-14. She has been on BoardAssist's distribution list for years; some boards carry a large financial commitment. Question: is it worth joining one, and which?*

## Panel view — a qualified YES, to the right board only
- **CMO / Research (the upside):** a well-chosen arts or museum board puts Alina in a room of collectors, museum and foundation leaders, and the advisor ecosystem; builds credibility; is mission-aligned (public access); and, uniquely, gives her **primary observation of governance, sustainability, and succession from the inside**, the exact subject of the [[flagship-report]]. It also suits her relationships-as-byproduct temperament far better than cold prospecting.
- **CFO (the caution):** Nariway has earned $0. A large give-or-get (cultural boards can run $25K to $100K+/yr) is premature spend before a first paid engagement. Board work also competes for time with the two things that make money. And the cheaper, faster route to the same rooms is the [[warm-network]] + [[conversations-series|Conversations]], not yet used.
- **Guardrail:** **never join a board to prospect** fellow trustees; the return is credibility, learning, and slow relationships, not lead-gen.

## Criteria for a fit board (judge any candidate against these)
1. Arts / culture, ideally touching private collections entering public life.
2. A board thick with collectors, museum/foundation leaders, and the advisor ecosystem.
3. Where Alina's finance + strategy + governance is genuinely needed and valued (she is a standout trustee, not a checkbook).
4. **Low or no give-or-get** (skills as the contribution) given the $0-revenue picture. Save big-money trophy boards for when there is income.
5. Mission-aligned (public access to art).
6. A research dividend, an institution actually grappling with sustainability or succession.

**Avoid:** off-thesis civic/social-service boards; any large financial minimum until Nariway earns.

## Recommendation
Run the cheap evidence-phase moves first. Take a **low-cost, high-relevance** board now if one appears in the database; defer any big-give board until there is revenue. Any material financial commitment goes to the [[advisory-panel]] and the [[cfo-brief|CFO]] before Alina commits.
