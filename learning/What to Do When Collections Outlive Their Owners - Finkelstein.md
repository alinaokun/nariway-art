Barry W. Finkelstein (San Jose estate-planning attorney, 26 years advising Silicon Valley wealth), "What to Do When Collections Outlive Their Owners" (Trusts & Estates / WealthManagement.com, published Jul 20, 2026). A named practitioner's own case study, not a generic framework piece: a successor trustee, "Peter," steps in to administer the estate of an 82-year-old Silicon Valley executive who left behind 26 vehicles and assorted automobilia (stored at the house and in two climate-controlled garages), a ski lodge, an apartment building, a pied-à-terre in the south of France, artwork, and other collections, with no plan for any of it. Walks through valuation, distribution, and the practical chaos of a mixed collection nobody planned an exit for — about as concrete an illustration of Nariway's own core question as the trusts-and-estates trade press gets.
