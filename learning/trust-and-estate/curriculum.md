# Trust & Estate Law — learning curriculum

*Alina's structured self-study of the trust-and-estate law that touches art in an estate. Part of the Knowledge environment (supports the business, is not the business). Companion to the standing legal watch in [[legal]] and the daily "Law & Estates" beat in [[nariway-signals]].*

## The goal (depth)
Operating literacy, not a law degree. The target is to **hold a substantive conversation with a trusts-and-estates attorney, follow the issues in a real matter, and know exactly when to route a question to counsel.** This builds credibility with referral sources and competence inside a matter. It does not make Alina a lawyer.

## The boundary (non-negotiable)
Alina and Nariway do **not** practice law or give legal advice. This learning exists so Nariway can coordinate competently and spot the issues, then bring in the right professional. Every note distinguishes three things, per the accuracy discipline:
1. **The law** — what a statute or authority actually requires (and in which jurisdiction).
2. **Common practice** — what sophisticated fiduciaries and advisors typically do.
3. **Nariway's role** — where Nariway helps (organize, compare, document, reconcile), and where it must not go.
Where a real answer needs a lawyer, the note says so.

## How it runs
Self-paced and manual. When Alina picks a topic, Claude researches it and drafts a sourced note (verified against authorities, never fabricated), which Alina reviews and keeps. Before a specific EPC event or attorney meeting, Claude can prepare a short briefing. No recurring autonomous agent, per the automation policy in [[agent-inventory]].

## The modules
*Ordered roughly by relevance to a Nariway matter. Status: not started / drafting / done. Each links to its note(s) as they are written.*

| # | Module | Why it matters to Nariway | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Estate administration lifecycle** — probate, appointment of the personal representative, marshaling assets, inventory, debts and taxes, distribution, closing | the map the whole matter sits on | not started |
| 2 | **The personal representative's authority and duties over art** — powers to possess and sell tangible property, fiduciary duties, self-dealing, court approval | the moment Nariway exists for | **done → [[executor-authority-over-art]]** |
| 3 | **Fiduciary duties in depth** — care, loyalty, impartiality, prudence; the prudent-investor / prudent-administrator standard applied to illiquid, non-income art | the standard every decision is judged against | not started |
| 4 | **How art passes** — specific vs. general bequests of tangible personal property, ademption, residuary, revocable trusts, tangible-property memoranda | determines what can even be sold or must go to a named heir | not started |
| 5 | **Estate tax and valuation of art** — Form 706, fair market value, qualified appraisal and qualified-appraiser rules, the IRS Art Advisory Panel, blockage discounts | the number everything keys off, and the appraisal chain Nariway organizes | not started |
| 6 | **Charitable disposition of art** — income and estate charitable deductions, the related-use rule, appraisal substantiation, fractional gifts, private foundations, donor-restricted gifts, deaccession | the "give it away" pathway and its tax mechanics | not started |
| 7 | **Donor intent and restrictions** — conditional gifts, cy pres, the Barnes lineage | why intent does not always survive, a core Nariway theme | not started |
| 8 | **Selling estate art prudently** — the fiduciary's process: appraisal, method selection, competing proposals, documentation, conflicts | the disposition workflow Nariway makes comparable and documented | not started |
| 9 | **Conflicts of interest and independence** — for the fiduciary, and for Nariway's own positioning | the spine of Nariway's independence claim | not started |
| 10 | **State procedure: New Jersey (Title 3B) and New York (EPTL + SCPA, Surrogate's Court)** | Alina's home geographies and first markets | not started |
| 11 | **The professional ecosystem and standards** — ACTEC, STEP, NAEPC, ABA RPTE, USPAP | who the players are and what standards bind them | not started |
