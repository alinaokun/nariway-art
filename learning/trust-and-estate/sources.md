# Trust & estate law — authoritative sources

*Where to learn from, so notes rest on real authority. Prefer primary law and official sources. Confirm the current edition or section before relying on any of these in a real matter.*

## Primary law
- **Uniform Probate Code (UPC)** — the model code many states track for estate administration and personal-representative powers. Uniform Law Commission (uniformlaws.org). Useful for the general framework even where a state has not adopted it wholesale.
- **New York** — the **Estates, Powers and Trusts Law (EPTL)** (esp. EPTL 11-1.1, fiduciaries' powers) and the **Surrogate's Court Procedure Act (SCPA)**. New York estates are administered in **Surrogate's Court**.
- **New Jersey** — **Title 3B**, Administration of Estates, Decedents and Others (NJ adopted a modified UPC). Probate through the **Surrogate's Court** in each county.
- **Federal tax** — **IRS Form 706** (United States Estate Tax Return) and its instructions; **IRC §2031/§2032** (valuation of the gross estate, alternate valuation); **IRC §170** (charitable deduction); **Treas. Reg. §20.2031-6** (valuation of household and personal effects, including art) and the **qualified appraisal** rules (Treas. Reg. §1.170A-16/17); **IRS Publication 561**, Determining the Value of Donated Property.

## The valuation / appraisal chain
- **IRS Art Appraisal Services and the Art Advisory Panel** — the IRS unit and panel of experts that reviews art valuations on estate and gift returns above a dollar threshold. Read the panel's annual reports.
- **USPAP** — Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice, The Appraisal Foundation (appraisalfoundation.org). The standard a qualified appraiser works to.

## Professional bodies and their materials
- **ACTEC** — American College of Trust and Estate Counsel (actec.org). Commentaries, podcasts, and the ACTEC Commentaries on the Model Rules.
- **ABA Real Property, Trust and Estate Law Section (RPTE)** — Probate & Property magazine, resources.
- **STEP** — Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners. A credential and a community; STEP New York runs relevant programming.
- **NAEPC** — National Association of Estate Planners & Councils, and the local Estate Planning Councils (the GTM channel).

## Treatises and study texts
- **Dukeminier & Sitkoff, *Wills, Trusts, and Estates*** — the standard survey; the fastest way to the whole field.
- **Loring and Rounds, *A Trustee's Handbook*** — the practitioner reference on trustee duties and powers.
- A **nutshell / hornbook** on wills and trusts for quick grounding before the survey.

## Art-specific
- **Center for Art Law** (itsartlaw.org) — case law and analysis at the art/law seam.
- **Lerner & Bresler, *Art Law*** — the standard art-law treatise (already in [[learning]]).
- Law-firm private-client alerts on art and estates (Day Pitney, Withers, Loeb, Grossman LLP's Art Law Blog), surfaced through the Signals "Law & Estates" beat.
