Kayli A. Callahan, University of Pennsylvania Law Review, Vol. 174 (Jul 12, 2026). A rigorous, current academic treatment arguing current museum deaccessioning self-regulation (AAMD standards plus ordinary nonprofit law) lacks real teeth, and proposing a hybrid framework of statutory/regulatory limits plus heightened judicial review. The most substantive new piece found on the institutional ("donate/lend to museum") branch of the succession decision tree this cycle — read alongside [[Deaccessioning and Its Discontents - Gammon]].
