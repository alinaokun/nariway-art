*Identify-level (not deep-research). Merged and deduped from two sweeps + the [[landscape-sweep]] 12. > **Verification status — read before this feeds any conclusion.** Every row is **Provisional** (a research *lead*) until checked against primary sources. **Major methodological find this run (not a candidate row, feeds [[claims-register]] and Part D directly):** a dedicated WebSearch discovery pass surfaced a real, peer-reviewed academic source this evidence base has been missing since H3's earliest "survivorship bias" flag — Olav Velthuis & Marton Gera, "The fragility of cultural philanthropy: why private art museums close," *International Journal of Cultural Policy* 31(6), 2024, built on a new database of private-museum closures worldwide (drawing on Larry's List's private-museum data): **76 of 523 private modern/contemporary-art museums identified worldwide had closed** (14.6%), with a **median operating life of no more than 10 years** before closure, closures driven mainly by financial reasons. This is the actual population-level base rate this dataset's own "would flip it further" test has asked for repeatedly and never had — see [[what-we-now-believe]] for this run's Part D use of it and [[claims-register]] for the citation.
