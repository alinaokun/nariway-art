## Narrative
CAM Raleigh is the sample's cleanest **distress** counterpart to [[rubin-museum]]'s strategic, well-capitalized building exit. Both institutions gave up a purpose-adapted building in 2024–2026; the resemblance ends there. Rubin closed from strength (reported $150M+, now $198.6M-ish net assets) with an explicitly stated global-reach rationale. CAM closed from weakness: revenue nearly halved over seven years (from ~$900K to under $570K), the museum shut its doors "temporarily" in June 2025 citing financial strain, and the building went up for sale over a year later with CBRE explicitly declining to set an asking price and instead letting "the market dictate." No founder, no endowment cushion of any real size, and a contested net-assets figure that this session could not resolve to even a single number. Framed by CAM's own leadership as freeing the organization "to invest in artists, ideas, and experiences instead of a mortgage" — the same strategic language Rubin used — but arriving from an acknowledged multi-year revenue decline rather than a position of strength, which is exactly the distinction [[rubin-museum]]'s Batch 7 pattern note flagged the coded schema as currently unable to make.
