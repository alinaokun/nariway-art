# Soloviev Foundation Gallery

One-line: Sheldon Solow built one of New York's great private collections over ~60 years but kept it famously closed to the public inside his 9 West 57th Street tower; after his 2020 death his son and widow opened it as the free Soloviev Foundation Gallery in 2023. A clean private-to-public transition driven by heirs (the anchor Botticelli was sold at auction in 2021 before the opening).
