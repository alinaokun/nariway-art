# Kiran Nadar Museum of Art (KNMA)

One-line: The wife of an Indian tech billionaire opened South Asia's first private museum of modern and contemporary art in 2010 from her own collection, and is now completing a vastly larger, David Adjaye-designed flagship campus expected to open in 2026 — a major living-founder standalone-museum case this dataset had not yet coded, and India's second entry after [[jaipur-centre-for-art]].

*(Public projection fields are in the frontmatter above, per [[case-template]]. The prose below is the internal research record.)*

**Why in the sample:** a genuinely major, currently-developing case surfaced in this run's "new museum openings 2026" discovery sweep — comparable in scale and architectural ambition to [[lucas-museum]] and [[mowaa]] (both also 2026-dated, marquee-architect builds this dataset already tracks). Distinct governance shape from most of this dataset's standalone-museum cases: KNMA is not an independent legal entity in its own right but operates under the umbrella of the Shiv Nadar Foundation, a large family philanthropy (over $1.2-1.5B invested across education, art, and culture per Foundation sourcing) — closer to [[wolfsonian-fiu]]'s university-backed model or a corporate foundation than a solo founder-only vehicle, though Kiran Nadar herself is both the collector and the museum's Chairperson.

## Narrative (sourced)

Kiran Nadar, an art collector and philanthropist married to HCL Technologies founder and billionaire Shiv Nadar, opened the Kiran Nadar Museum of Art in 2010 as India's first private museum dedicated to modern and contemporary art [secondary: Global Indian, KNMA]. The museum operates across two current Delhi-NCR sites totaling roughly 50,000 sq ft, drawing on a personal collection exceeding 7,000 works spanning the major phases of 20th-century and contemporary Indian art, alongside younger contemporary practice [secondary: KNMA]. The museum is backed by the Shiv Nadar Foundation, established in 1994, which describes having invested $1.2-1.5B (figures vary by source) across seven institutions spanning education, art, and culture; Kiran Nadar is both a Foundation trustee and the museum's Chairperson [secondary: Shiv Nadar Foundation, KNMA governance page]. In 2023, star architect David Adjaye (also the architect behind [[mowaa]] in Benin City) unveiled designs for a vastly larger flagship building — a 100,000-square-metre campus near Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport, intended to combine visual art with music, dance, and theatre under one roof and described as India's largest cultural centre [secondary: Dezeen, The Art Newspaper, Elle Decor India]. As of this run's sourcing, the new building is scheduled to open in 2026, though an exact date was not located.

## Gaps / contradictions

- Sourcing gives inconsistent Shiv Nadar Foundation cumulative investment figures ($1.2B vs. $1.5B across different outlets); neither figure is KNMA-specific, and this dataset should not treat either as an endowment figure for the museum itself.
- David Adjaye's continued high-profile role here, following his firm's work on the now-stalled MOWAA project in Nigeria, is a coincidence worth flagging but not (on current sourcing) evidence of any shared risk between the two projects.
- No primary financial disclosure was locatable this run (WebFetch remains `EGRESS_BLOCKED`; India's nonprofit filing regime was not identified as a WebSearch-accessible equivalent to the US 990/ProPublica system this dataset otherwise relies on for `secondary`-tier figures).
