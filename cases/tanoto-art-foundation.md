# Tanoto Art Foundation (museum forthcoming, Singapore)

One-line: A living Singaporean collector's foundation is converting into a purpose-built public contemporary-art museum - but as one line item inside a Kengo Kuma-designed, family-backed commercial tower redevelopment on Orchard Road, rather than as its own standalone campus. A variant of the found-standalone-museum pathway worth tracking precisely because it stresses the taxonomy (see [[candidate-universe]]).

Belinda Tanoto's Tanoto Art Foundation is set to run a roughly 2,800-square-metre contemporary-art museum inside the redevelopment of the Tanglin Shopping Centre on Orchard Road, a Kengo Kuma-designed mixed-use tower backed by the Tanoto family's real-estate arm and reportedly a redevelopment of significant scale. The museum was announced by Singapore's culture minister at the Patron of the Arts Awards and is due by 2030; the foundation already runs public exhibitions and symposia. Coded here as an emerging build-institution / found-standalone-museum case, with the distinguishing feature that the public museum is embedded in a commercial development rather than built as a dedicated campus.
