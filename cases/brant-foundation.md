# The Brant Foundation

One-line: Peter Brant built one of the largest private holdings of postwar and contemporary American art (from a teenage Warhol purchase in 1967) and opened it to the public through a foundation, a converted Greenwich barn (2009) and a repurposed East Village substation (2019), keeping the collection privately owned.
