# The Bunnen Collection of Photography (High Museum of Art)

One-line: An Atlanta photographer-collector's 1981 core gift founded the High Museum of Art's photography collection and, through decades of continued support, built a department now named for her — a collection that created an institutional capability, not just an accession.

*(Public projection fields are in the frontmatter above, per [[case-template]]. Added 2026-09-07 from the High Museum harvest, see [[museum-harvest]].)*

Lucinda W. Bunnen (1930-2022) collected photography when it was still a young museum field. Her core gift of more than 650 works in 1981 established the High Museum's photography collection, and her continued acquisition support over the following decades built the department, whose galleries carry her name.

*Sources: High Museum of Art (Bunnen Collection exhibition; museum history); ArtsATL obituary and profile.*
