# The Bunnen Collection of Photography (High Museum of Art)

One-line: An Atlanta photographer-collector's 1981 gift formed the core of the High Museum of Art's photography holdings and, through decades of continued support, helped build a department now named for her — a collection that strengthened an institutional capability, not just an accession.

Lucinda W. Bunnen (1930-2022) collected photography when it was still a young museum field. The High had begun collecting photography in 1973, and her 1981 gift formed the core of its holdings; her continued acquisition support over the following decades built a collection of more than 650 works, in a department whose galleries carry her name.
