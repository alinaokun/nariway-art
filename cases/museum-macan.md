# Museum MACAN (Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art in Nusantara)

One-line: Haryanto Adikoesoemo, chairman of the Indonesian conglomerate AKR Corporindo, spent 25 years assembling roughly 800 works of Indonesian and international modern and contemporary art, established a dedicated operating foundation in 2015, and opened Museum MACAN — Indonesia's first museum devoted to modern and contemporary art — in Jakarta in November 2017.

**Why in the sample:** closes a real geographic gap this dataset has flagged repeatedly (Southeast Asia beyond Singapore/UOB); a corporate-adjacent private fortune (an energy/chemicals conglomerate, structurally similar to [[monte-dei-paschi-collection]]'s and this sample's bank cases, but here funding a personal rather than corporate collection) building the region's first major private museum for a specifically underserved field — modern and contemporary Indonesian art had no dedicated public home before this. Also a pre-opening governance lock-in case (foundation established two years before the building opened), the same shape as [[fondation-beyeler]] and [[muzej-lah]], now with a fourth country.

## Narrative
Haryanto Adikoesoemo, chairman of AKR Corporindo, an Indonesian chemicals-logistics and energy conglomerate, spent roughly 25 years collecting modern and contemporary art before opening Museum MACAN (Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art in Nusantara) in Jakarta in November 2017 — Indonesia's first museum dedicated specifically to that field. The ~800-work collection spans Indonesian and international artists (roughly half Indonesian), reflecting an explicit dual mission: showing international modern-art history to Indonesian audiences while nurturing the country's own contemporary scene through acquisition and education programs. The museum's operating foundation, Yayasan Museum MACAN, was established in mid-2015, two years before the building opened — a pre-opening governance lock-in in the same shape this sample has already found in Switzerland ([[fondation-beyeler]]) and Slovenia ([[muzej-lah]]), though co-founder Sintawati Ongkowijoyo's role and whether any board members exist beyond the founding family were not established this run. The museum drew roughly 90,000 visitors in its first three months and has since built a substantial education program reaching schools across most of Indonesia's provinces. Indonesia has no equivalent of a US Form 990 or comparable nonprofit-disclosure regime, so financial durability figures (net assets, true endowment, expenses) are `unknown` for a structural reason, the same category this sample already tracks for China, Kazakhstan, and Georgia — not a gap a re-fetch will resolve.
