# Souls Grown Deep Foundation

One-line: William Arnett's ~1,000-work collection of Black vernacular art of the American South (Gee's Bend, Thornton Dial) is dispersed *into* the canon — gifts/purchases to 20+ major museums — plus advocacy; thriving past the founder's 2020 death.

**Why in the sample:** the **anti-monument**. Distinctiveness and public-benefit *are* the thesis, not market value — the purest expression of the north star, and a completed non-building pathway.
