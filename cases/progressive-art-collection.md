## Narrative
Progressive is the sample's first corporate case built around a **named, deceased individual founder inside a public company**, rather than either an anonymous institutional program (Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase's founding is credited to David Rockefeller but reads institutionally) or a foundation-funded museum (Fondation Cartier, Fondazione Prada). Peter Lewis started buying art personally in 1974, the same year he took over as Progressive's CEO; hired his ex-wife Toby Devan Lewis as full-time curator in 1985 (a governance detail worth noting for H8 — an unusually personal, informal succession arrangement inside a Fortune 500 company); and by the time of his 2013 death had built one of the most-cited corporate collections in the US. What makes this case useful for the disconfirmation work Alina asked for is what happened *next*: neither the founder's 2013 death nor the founding curator's own 2022 death interrupted the program. Progressive's own public materials describe an active, ongoing acquisitions and workplace-display program in 2026, run by corporate staff, funded from ordinary operating budgets, with no dedicated endowment, no foundation, and no binding gift instrument of any kind. This is a governance-continuity case that looks nothing like the sample's collector-museum survivors (independent self-perpetuating boards, locked succession) — it survived through the far simpler mechanism of being an ordinary corporate line item inside a large, stable, ongoing business, immune to the death of any one person because no one person's continued existence was ever structurally necessary to its operation. That is a different *kind* of durability than [[case-template]]'s governance vocabulary was built to describe (independent-board vs. family-controlled vs. founder-sole), worth flagging as a fifth governance-continuity mechanism alongside the four the dataset has already surfaced (self-perpetuating board, locked family succession, permanence-doctrine pre-decision, and now: absorption into an ordinary going-concern's operating budget).
