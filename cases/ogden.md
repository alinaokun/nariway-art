## Narrative

Roger H. Ogden, a New Orleans real-estate developer and philanthropist, assembled by the early 1990s a collection of roughly 1,500 paintings and sculptures by more than 500 Southern artists; a founding gift of about 600 works became the core of the Ogden Museum of Southern Art [source: https://www.nola.com/news/business/article_c8b10f19-82bb-5403-b2d6-a38688ac11d1.html]. The museum was established in 1999 in partnership with the University of New Orleans and civic leaders, occupying a purpose-built modernist hall (Stephen Goldring Hall, opened 2003) alongside the adapted 1889 H.H. Richardson-designed library on Camp Street in the Warehouse Arts District [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogden_Museum_of_Southern_Art]. Its thesis is tight and regional — the art of the American South across some fifteen states and all media — and the holdings have since grown to a contested "more than 4,000" to "more than 5,000" works [source: https://www.nola.com/news/business/article_c8b10f19-82bb-5403-b2d6-a38688ac11d1.html]. The university affiliation, which gave the young museum institutional ballast, became its central friction: state budget cuts to UNO threatened the link, and Ogden ultimately sued the UNO Foundation to move ownership of the art to the museum nonprofit and reclaim works he had loaned [source: https://www.nola.com/news/business/unos-link-to-ogden-museum-of-southern-art-threatened-by-state-budget-cuts/article_00a3a79b-acaf-5800-976a-442d7c2d2d0b.html]. Financially the institution is thin: FY2024 net assets of $5.68M against $3.54M of expenses give a net-assets-to-opex ratio of only about 1.6x (an upper bound, as net assets include building and collection, not a true endowment) [source: https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/721479496]. Reserves also contracted sharply — from $9.78M in FY2021 to $5.68M in FY2024 — a decline that needs a cause before any durability read is firm [source: https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/721479496]. The founder remains living and active, pledging an estimated $20M more to the museum in December 2023 [source: https://www.nola.com/news/business/article_c8b10f19-82bb-5403-b2d6-a38688ac11d1.html]. The case is coded stable/moderate: durable in operation and mission, but under-endowed and structurally contested.
