## Narrative
Gap Inc. co-founders Donald and Doris Fisher assembled one of the largest US private collections of modern and contemporary art. Their plan for a standalone museum in San Francisco's Presidio met community opposition and was dropped; in 2009 the family instead placed the collection on long-term loan to SFMOMA, retaining ownership. SFMOMA states the term was extended to 100 years with 25-year renewals, covering 720-plus works by 100 artists. Donald Fisher died weeks after the 2009 agreement; the collection anchored SFMOMA's 2016 expansion in dedicated galleries, and a full reinstallation was announced April 2026, weeks before Doris Fisher's death at 94. The arrangement keeps ownership with the family while guaranteeing a century of public display inside a major institution, so it has already outlasted both founders.
