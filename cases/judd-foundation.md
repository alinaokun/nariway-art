# Judd Foundation

One-line: Donald Judd's will (1996) created a foundation to preserve his own New York and Marfa living and working spaces and the art permanently installed in them; 101 Spring Street opened to the public by reservation in 2013. An artist-legacy foundation, distinct from the [[chinati]] museum Judd founded in Marfa in 1986.
