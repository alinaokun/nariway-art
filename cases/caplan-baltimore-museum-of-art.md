## Narrative
Constance R. Caplan has been engaged with the Baltimore Museum of Art since 1955 and previously served as its board chair; her personal contemporary-art collection was the subject of a dedicated exhibition at the Art Institute of Chicago in 2020 [secondary: Baltimore Fishbowl, prabook]. In July 2026 the BMA announced it had acquired approximately 118 works over the preceding six months, including 64 gifted directly by Caplan — works by Janine Antoni, Hilla and Bernd Becher, John Chiara, Fernanda Gomes, Robert Morris, Stephen Parrino, Nobuo Sekine, Richard Tuttle, Lee Ufan, Jack Whitten, and Lisa Yuskavage among others [secondary: Artforum, Artdaily, Baltimore Fishbowl].
