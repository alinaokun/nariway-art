One-line: Czech entrepreneur Karel Komárek backs 0xCollection, a Basel-based institution founded in 2023 under curator/director Elle Anastasiou to collect, exhibit, and preserve new-media and digital art from 1960 to the present — operating with no permanent public venue of its own, instead touring commissioned works and partnering with existing institutions (Prague, 2023; the Kennedy Center, 2024).

## Why in the sample

Every prior "loans-only, no building" case in this dataset (Onera, Thoma, Souls Grown Deep) circulates *physical* objects between existing museum walls. 0xCollection circulates a different kind of asset — commissioned digital, AI-generated, and time-based work that may have no single physical instantiation at all — through a touring/partnership model that is structurally similar (no dedicated building) but categorically distinct (the medium itself resists the physical-object assumptions built into fields like `collection_size_current` and `building_type`). It is also the sample's clearest test of whether a wealthy individual backer (rather than the backer collecting personally) founding an institution *around a field* rather than around their own acquisitions changes the coded shape — Komárek is described as backing the institution, not as the collector of the works inside it, a decision-owner ambiguity flagged below.

## Narrative (sourced)

0xCollection launched in Basel in 2023 with the financial backing of Karel Komárek, a Czech entrepreneur, investor, and philanthropist whose KKCG conglomerate spans energy, lottery/gaming, and technology holdings, and whose separately organized Karel Komárek Family Foundation pursues broader philanthropy [source: KKCG.com; confidence: secondary]. The institution operates under the direction of curator and cultural strategist Elle Anastasiou, and is dedicated to developing and preserving new-media and time-based artworks dating from 1960 to the present, framed by its own materials as a "multidisciplinary platform spanning exhibition, research, and education" [source: Outland Art; ARTnews; confidence: secondary]. Its name — a reference to the "0x" hexadecimal prefix used to denote color values in digital files — signals the collecting focus explicitly, distinguishing it from every other institution in this sample by medium rather than by geography, founder biography, or governance structure.

0xCollection presented its inaugural public exhibition in Prague in September 2023, including a commissioned work by media artist Refik Anadol alongside a group show of eight contemporary artists [source: ARTnews, 2023; confidence: secondary]. In 2024 it partnered with the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts in Washington, D.C. to stage the federal institution's first-ever digital-art exhibition — a notable placement, since it demonstrates the collection's touring model successfully reaching a major public institution outside its home base, a form of public access this sample's other loans-only cases achieve with physical objects but 0xCollection achieves with a fundamentally different, less easily inventoried medium [source: Kennedy Center program page via search synthesis; Live Design; confidence: secondary].
