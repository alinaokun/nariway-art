# Neue Galerie → The Met

One-line: Ronald Lauder's museum of German & Austrian modernism (a tight single thesis) announced in May 2026 that it will **merge into The Metropolitan Museum in 2028**, with the building, collection, and programming continuing under The Met — choosing continuity over indefinite independence.

**Why in the sample:** a distinct succession solution — **hand off to a major institution** rather than go it alone. A live, modern H8 case (legacy vs. control). **✓ Verified** (both institutions announced May 2026).
