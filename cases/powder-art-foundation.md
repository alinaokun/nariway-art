# Powder Art Foundation (Powder Mountain)

One-line: Netflix co-founder Reed Hastings, owner of the Powder Mountain ski resort in Eden, Utah, bankrolled a new independent nonprofit, the Powder Art Foundation, to build a skiable open-air art museum with large-scale works by Jenny Holzer, James Turrell, Nancy Holt, and others — then deliberately took no official role in the organization he funded.

**Why in the sample:** a clean, contemporary instance of Pattern 1's governance-beats-endowment finding forming in real time, before any death or succession test: an extremely well-resourced living founder (net worth reported in the billions) chose independent-board governance and personal recusal *at founding*, the same architecture this dataset's strongest survivors (Fondation Beyeler, Storm King's later generations) arrived at only through decades of practice or crisis. A genuinely novel resort-museum hybrid form, and a second Dia Art Foundation partnership in the sample after [[dia-art-foundation]] itself, this time as an external curatorial collaborator rather than the primary institution.

## Narrative (sourced)

Reed Hastings, Netflix's co-founder, owns Powder Mountain, a large ski resort in Eden, Utah. In August 2024 he announced plans for a multi-use, open-air art museum across the resort's grounds, forming the nonprofit Powder Art Foundation to build and run it [secondary: [Robb Report](https://robbreport.com/travel/destinations/reed-hastings-powder-mountain-museum-1235813264/); [Powder](https://www.powder.com/news/powder-mountain-art-museum)]. Large-scale, site-specific installations by Jenny Holzer, Nancy Holt, James Turrell, and Paul McCarthy were announced, with pieces by Holt, Nobuo Sekine, EJ Hill, and Kayode Ojo already installed by 2025-2026 [secondary: [Galerie](https://galeriemagazine.com/utahs-powder-mountain-unveils-inaugural-suite-of-outdoor-artworks/); [Archpaper](https://www.archpaper.com/2025/10/art-installation-utah-skiable-outdoor-art-museum/)]. The works are viewable on foot in summer and fall (free public access for hiking) and by ski in winter for resort guests [secondary: [Artnet News](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/powder-mountain-land-art-2703100)]. Landscape architecture is by Reed Hilderbrand, the firm behind Storm King Art Center's own grounds; a future visitor center is designed by Johnston Marklee, architects of the MCA Chicago [secondary: Robb Report].

The governance detail is the case's strongest finding: the Foundation is guided by a five-person board with Alexandra Magnuson as executive director, and while "Hastings bankrolled" its creation, he "now has no official role within the organization" [secondary: search-synthesized, sourced to multiple outlets including a GuideStar profile snippet, EIN 99-4005610]. This is a founding-moment governance choice, not a later correction forced by crisis — closer to Fondation Beyeler's "straight to an independent board, no family-continuity option" pattern than to the more common trajectory in this dataset of sole control that only yields power under duress (Dia Art Foundation, Pyramid Hill) or at death (the majority of Pattern 1's survivors). In 2026 the Foundation announced a long-term partnership with the Dia Art Foundation, covering curatorial guidance and collection-sharing [secondary: Artnet News].
