# The FLAG Art Foundation

One-line: collector-philanthropists Glenn and Amanda Fuhrman created a free Chelsea contemporary-art space in 2008 as the public-facing exhibition arm of their private holdings; FLAG itself is deliberately non-collecting, showing curated exhibitions and works drawn from the separate Fuhrman Family Foundation.
