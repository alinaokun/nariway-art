## Narrative
Sandy Lerner co-founded Cisco Systems and later founded the cosmetics label Urban Decay; from her Ayrshire Farm estate in Upperville, Virginia, she spent more than three decades assembling what Bonhams describes as one of the most significant private collections of cat-related decorative arts, books, and collectibles ever assembled, spanning ancient objects to 20th-century fine art, and documented in her 2017 book *Caticons: 4,000 Years of Art Imitating Cats* [secondary: [ARTnews](https://www.artnews.com/art-news/news/sandy-lerner-cat-themed-art-collection-auctioned-bonhams-1234795289/)]. In 2026 she began dispersing the collection, nearly 1,000 lots, across four Bonhams sales (two live, two online) in September and October, split between Bonhams' new US flagship at 111 West 57th Street in New York and a Massachusetts venue. The first live sale, Part I, on September 30 in New York, includes 66 works, decorative objects, jewelry, and collectibles, headlined by Léonard Tsuguharu Foujita's *Chat et Chaton* (1934, estimate $30,000–$50,000), Diego Giacometti's *Tête de Chat* (circa 1970, estimate $20,000–$30,000), and Henri Matisse's *Visages et grenades* (circa 1952, estimate $10,000–$15,000) [secondary: Ocula, Artsy].

**Relevance to the evidence base.** This is a useful counter-example inside the sample's own `sell-auction` pathway: unlike [[ganz-collection]], [[herbert-lust-collection]], or [[blaquier-collection]] (this run), where the sale followed the collector's death, Lerner is dispersing her own collection while alive and evidently well-resourced — a deliberate personal choice, not a default triggered by an absent coordinator. It also tests H4/H5 (distinctiveness predicting public-institution potential) from an unusual angle: the collection is about as tightly-coherent as this sample has coded (a single subject across four millennia), yet no public institution or foundation was ever built around it, even by a founder with the resources and institution-building experience (Lerner has separately funded the nonprofit Ayrshire Farm and welfare causes) to do so — distinctiveness alone did not produce an institutional pathway here, a data point for keeping H4/H5 to its own claim (coherence predicting *durability once public*, not predicting *whether a founder chooses to institutionalize at all*).
