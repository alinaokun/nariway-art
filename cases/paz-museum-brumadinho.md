# Bernardo Paz's new museum, Brumadinho

One-line: Bernardo Paz — who founded Inhotim, then lost sole operating control of it during a 2016–2020 governance and financial crisis this dataset's own [[inhotim]] file already documents — is now building a second, separate art park next door, on his own renewed personal control, with the same principal advisor (Allan Schwartzman) and the same underlying model.

**Why in the sample:** the sample's first direct test of *founder recidivism* after a governance failure — does a founder who has already lived through the consequence H6/H8 predicts (founder-sole control creating a collapse risk) design differently the second time, or rebuild the identical structure? Coverage located this run names no foundation, board, or governing entity at all for the new project, which — if it holds once the museum actually opens and its legal structure becomes locatable — would repeat rather than correct the Inhotim pattern. Too early to code as confirming or disconfirming anything; flagged here as a case to watch, and a candidate future H6/H8 test once a governing structure is announced or the founder's stated succession plan (if any) becomes public.

## Narrative (sourced)

Bernardo Paz founded Inhotim, one of the world's largest private art parks, but lost personal operating control of it during a 2016–2020 financial and governance crisis that this dataset's own [[inhotim]] case documents as a *voluntary* total donation while Paz was still living — a resolution closer to [[dia-art-foundation]]'s forced-handoff pattern than to a Terra-style collapse, but a governance-vacuum crisis all the same. In July 2026, press reported Paz is now developing an entirely separate museum and botanical garden — reported budget figures diverge sharply between roughly $195 million (Wallpaper, blooloop) and R$1 billion (Newcity Brazil), unreconciled at any plausible exchange rate — on a 311-acre site directly adjacent to Inhotim in Brumadinho, Minas Gerais, for his own art collection of roughly 3,000 South American and international contemporary works [secondary: [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2026/07/31/bernardo-paz-inhotim-founder-new-museum-next-door); [Artforum](https://www.artforum.com/news/collector-bernardo-paz-developing-new-museum-in-brazil-1234756249/); [ARTnews](https://www.artnews.com/art-news/news/inhotim-founder-bernardo-paz-namesake-museum-1234794596/)]. The curatorial team includes Allan Schwartzman — Inhotim's own co-founder and Paz's long-standing principal advisor — alongside Paulo Miyada and Fernanda Arruda [secondary: same sources]. Forty gallery/pavilion spaces are planned; first pavilions, showing work by Michael Heizer, Anna Maria Maiolino, and Sonia Gomes, are slated to open September 2027 [secondary: [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/museu-bernardo-paz-brazil)]. No source located this run names any foundation, trust, or nonprofit entity behind the new project — as of this writing it reads as a personally-financed, personally-controlled undertaking, the same governance shape Inhotim itself began with.
