## Narrative
Fondazione Prada extends the sample's corporate-adjacent category in a genuinely new direction. Every other corporate case coded so far (Lehman, Enron, Deutsche Bank, JPMorgan Chase, Credit Suisse→UBS, and this run's own [[ubs-art-collection]]) is a **collection owned by the corporation itself**, displayed in its own offices, with no independent legal identity as a public cultural institution and no named individual founder. Fondazione Prada is the opposite construction: an independent, purpose-built public museum with a genuine founder-decider (Miuccia Prada, with Patrizio Bertelli), that happens to be funded by an ongoing corporate sponsorship relationship with the fashion house they lead, rather than by a personal fortune placed into an independent endowment the way [[fondation-beyeler]] or [[chinati]] were. This makes it a distinct fourth funding architecture for `found-foundation` cases in the sample: **personal-endowment-funded** (Beyeler, Chinati, Glenstone), **postal-clerk-salary-funded** (Vogel), **corporate-collection-owned** (the bank cases), and now **ongoing-corporate-sponsorship-funded** (Prada) — a foundation with real institutional independence and a real founder, but no independent capital base of its own, dependent instead on a continuing decision by Prada Group's own leadership (who are also the Foundation's founders) to keep funding it each year.

That dependency is untested in this sample precisely because it has never been tested in reality: Prada Group remains profitable and Miuccia Prada and Patrizio Bertelli remain both alive and in control of the company, so the scenario this sample's other "sponsorship dependency" cases show playing out badly — Reader's Digest's founder-collection sold off to fund a struggling parent company (see [[readers-digest-wallace-collection]], coded this same run), or Deutsche Bank narrowing its dedicated venue while keeping the underlying collection — has no Fondazione Prada analogue yet. The 2023 Guidance Committee is the first documented step away from pure founder-sole control, formed on the Foundation's 30th anniversary rather than in response to any visible strain, which reads as proactive governance-broadening rather than crisis response — worth revisiting if or when Prada Group's own fortunes, or Miuccia Prada's own succession, become live questions.
