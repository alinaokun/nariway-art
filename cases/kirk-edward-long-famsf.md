## Narrative
Kirk Edward Long built, over decades, one of the most extensive private collections of 16th-century Mannerist prints in the world, more than 180 works from the collection were the subject of a dedicated museum exhibition ("Myth, Allegory and Faith"), and in 2016 the Cantor Arts Center at Stanford co-published an illustrated scholarly catalogue of his entire 700-work print collection, with essays by ten scholars [secondary: [Association of Print Scholars](https://printscholars.org/myth-allegory-and-faith-the-kirk-edward-long-collection-of-mannerist-prints/); [Cantor Arts Center](https://museum.stanford.edu/about/press-releases/extraordinary-private-collection-16th-century-works-view-first-time-public)]. His broader holdings extend to paintings, antiquities, and decorative arts, including a 19th-century Gustave Moreau painting and a 19th-century bust by Charles-Henri-Joseph Cordier.

In November 2025, the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco announced a gift from Long of 1,600 works, one of the largest single-owner gifts in the history of the Achenbach Foundation for Graphic Arts (founded 1948), and the third-largest single-owner donation of works on paper the Foundation has ever received [secondary: [ARTnews](https://www.artnews.com/list/art-news/news/famsf-gift-kirk-edward-long-1234759946/)]. The gift arrived inside FAMSF's multiyear "Gifts of Art" campaign, launched in 2022 to mark the 50th anniversary of the de Young/Legion of Honor merger and the Legion of Honor's 2024 centennial [secondary: [FAMSF press room](https://www.famsf.org/press-room/gifts-of-art)].
