# Petrucci Family Foundation Collection of African American Art

One-line: a New Jersey family foundation built a 500-plus-work collection of African American art with the explicit, stated purpose of lending it to museums rather than displaying it in a gallery of its own — a pure loan-circulation model with no building at all.

**Why in the sample:** a genuinely different non-museum pathway from this dataset's other loan cases (Fisher→SFMOMA is a single 100-year institutional loan; Bank of America's program is corporate) — a private family foundation that never built, and by its own account never intended to build, a home venue, choosing circulation itself as the public-access strategy from the outset. ## Narrative (sourced)

The Petrucci Family Foundation, a New Jersey private foundation established in the mid-2000s with a general mission of "supporting education and creating opportunity for Americans at every stage of and station of life," began collecting African American art specifically in 2012, stating that "we cannot truly understand American history without understanding African American history" [secondary: [Petrucci Family Foundation](https://www.petruccifamilyfoundation.org/); [GrantExec](https://grantexec.com/foundations/201601999)]. Unlike this dataset's other collector-founded entities, the Petrucci collection was built from the outset for circulation rather than display: it has never had, and by its own public materials does not plan, a dedicated gallery of its own. Works are lent instead to partner museums — recent and ongoing venues include the Hunterdon Art Museum, the Allentown Art Museum, and the David C. Driskell Center at the University of Maryland — with the Foundation's stated purpose being to expand the American art canon taught and exhibited nationally, not to build an institution bearing the family's name [secondary: [Hunterdon Art Museum](https://www.hunterdonartmuseum.org/exhibitions/petrucci-family-foundation/); [Allentown Art Museum](https://www.allentownartmuseum.org/exhibitions/an-essential-presence-the-petrucci-family-foundation-collection-of-african-american-art/); [Driskell Center](https://driskellcenter.umd.edu/news/petrucci-family-foundation-collection-african-american-art-and-david-c-driskell-center)]. The collection now numbers more than 500 works in all media [secondary: petruccifamilyfoundation.org].
